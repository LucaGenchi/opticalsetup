Highlight

Adds a colored background region behind the optical setup, for grouping components or calling attention to a section without changing any traced rays.

Open in the canvas →

A live trace, not a picture of one — but this preview is not interactive. Open it in the canvas to move things, change parameters, and save or export your own version.

In the real world

Shaded regions in optical diagrams can identify a subsystem, distinguish experimental stages, or mark an area of interest. Such visual grouping has no physical optical meaning unless the caption explicitly assigns one.

In OpticalSetup

Resize and position the highlight behind the components you want to group, then choose its appearance. It remains behind both rays and elements, so the beam path and hardware stay visible. Use a text label to explain what the shaded region means.

The example marks the lens area on a simple bench. Move or resize the highlight across the incoming and outgoing beam to confirm that only the composition changes.

Simplified vs. reality

A highlight is diagram-only. Its color and shape do not represent refractive index, absorption, an aperture, or a boundary between optical media.
