Patterns / Handling pulses and nonlinear interactions

PULSE-02 · Pattern family · Setup available

Dispersion compensation

Combine dispersive contributions to obtain a target pulse duration and spectral phase.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Catalogue glass GDD and quadratic Gaussian pulse propagation are modeled. The compressor is a native phase-compensation element; prism separation, higher-order phase and measured instrument response are not inferred.

The setup

Three native paths compare a 150 fs, 532 nm reference, propagation through 100 mm of N-SF11, and the same glass followed by −38680 fs² compensation. Autocorrelators read all three outputs.

  1. Compare the three autocorrelation durations using the same assumed Gaussian shape.
  2. Set the compensation to zero or reverse its sign and compare the third path with the glass-only path.

Components in this setup

  • Pulsed Laser
  • Autocorrelator
  • Detector screen
  • Glass rod
  • Pulse compressor

Arrangement and variants

Prism pairs, grating arrangements, chirped mirrors, or guided dispersive stages.

Design constraints

Higher-order dispersion, bandwidth, losses, and spatial chirp can remain after GDD compensation.
