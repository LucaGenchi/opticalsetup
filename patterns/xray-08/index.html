The example

An illustrative 13.5 nm EUV field uses multilayer-coated reflective optics through illumination, a reflective mask and a reduction relay. Each reflection contributes spectral/angle-dependent loss; no transmissive lens is implied.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 13.5 nm EUV source — Illumination is selected within the multilayer passband.
  2. Multilayer collector — Collects source light using a reflective coating.
  3. Reflective illuminator — Shapes illumination at the reflective mask.
  4. Reflective patterned mask — Encodes the exposure pattern by reflection.
  5. Reduction mirror relay — A multiple-mirror prescription images the mask at reduced size.
  6. Wafer image plane (Sample) — Receives the projected pattern; exposure dose requires all losses.
  7. Rejected out-of-band light — Spectral purity filtering excludes unwanted source bands.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Coordinated multilayer-coated mirrors, commonly within a vacuum optical path.

Key constraints

Bandwidth, incidence angle, contamination, and cumulative reflection loss constrain throughput.
