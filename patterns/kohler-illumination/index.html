ILL-01 · Optical layout · Setup available

Kohler illumination

Control illuminated field and illumination aperture through separate conjugate-plane sets.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. The three-point lamp is a discrete extended-source sample, not a uniform illumination calculation.

The setup

Three native point emitters sample an extended lamp. A 25.455 mm collector conjugates the lamp to the condenser front focal plane; the field stop is conjugate to the specimen camera.

  1. Compare the field stop at x = 140 mm with the angular aperture at x = 190 mm.
  2. Change either stop gap independently and inspect the admitted ray bundles.

Components in this setup

  • Point source
  • Thin convex lens
  • Slit
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Image the source into an aperture plane and the field stop into the specimen plane.

Design constraints

Both conjugate sets must be aligned; uniformity still depends on source and optical quality.
