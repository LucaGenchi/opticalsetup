Patterns / Illuminating and projecting

ILL-08 · Pattern family · Setup available

Multispot illumination

Distribute excitation or processing light across several target points.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Four geometric channels and focal positions are traced. A 2×2 spot grid, microlens diffraction and equal-irradiance calibration require a second transverse axis and wave modeling.

The setup

Four actual 50 mm lenslets form four focal spots in a line on a camera. This is a meridional four-spot illuminator rather than a simulated second transverse row.

  1. Count the four lenslets and follow each independent focus.
  2. Change one focal length to move that channel away from the shared focal plane.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Diffractive fan-out, microlens arrays, or programmable holographic splitting.

Design constraints

Power per spot, uniformity, crosstalk, and unwanted orders limit scaling.
