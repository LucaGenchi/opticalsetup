IMG-03 · Optical layout · Setup available

Pupil relay

Image a pupil onto an accessible optical plane.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. The objective pupil is represented by the receiving camera plane; this is the pupil-relay subsystem, not a full microscope.

The setup

A real galvo and 75/150 mm relay image its pivot onto a pupil-monitor camera. A 3 mm collimated beam expands to 6 mm in the relayed pupil.

  1. Read the pupil camera width with the galvo at zero mechanical angle.
  2. Change galvo angle slightly and compare pivot conjugation with changing the relay lens spacing.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Galvo mirror
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Relay optics between an entrance pupil and a scanner, stop, or corrector.

Design constraints

Pupil position and magnification must stay compatible with the field and apertures.
