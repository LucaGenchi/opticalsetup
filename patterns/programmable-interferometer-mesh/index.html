Programmable interferometer mesh

Compose tunable couplers and phase shifts into a multimode optical transformation.

No setup available

The registry has no guided 2×2 tunable interferometer cell, multiport waveguide junction or programmable mesh transfer model. A chain of free-space beamsplitters would not reproduce the specified four-mode mesh.

Arrangement and variants

Networks of Mach-Zehnder cells or equivalent tunable interferometers.

Design constraints

Loss accumulation, phase drift, calibration, and topology determine useful scale.
