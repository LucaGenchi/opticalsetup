CAV-05 · Optical layout · No setup available

Regenerative amplification

Trap a seed pulse for repeated amplification and then release it.

No setup available

There is no laser gain medium with round-trip regenerative amplification or pulse trapping/extraction across successive gated round trips. The seeded single-pass OPA does not provide cavity gain.

Arrangement and variants

Gain cavity with timed injection and extraction, commonly using polarization switching.

Design constraints

Switch timing, gain saturation, dispersion, and accumulated nonlinear phase matter.

References

  1. Coherent: regenerative amplifiers and ultrafast laser architecture

Used in

Ultrafast optics · Laser engineering · Materials processing and fabrication

Applications

Ultrashort-pulse amplification · high-energy pulse preparation

Related patterns

Cavity dumping (CAV-07); Chirped-pulse amplification (PULSE-01); Polarization-based send/return separation (ROUTE-04).

Combine with

Chirped-pulse amplification (PULSE-01) for managing peak intensity; Polarization-based send/return separation (ROUTE-04) for switched extraction.

What follows

Dispersion compensation (PULSE-02) in a CPA chain, then beam delivery.
