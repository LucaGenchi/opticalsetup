Reduction projection

Transfer a mask or modulator image to a target at reduced scale.

Schematic exampleFourfold reduction exposure

365 nm illuminator — Illuminate the mask field uniformly.

The example

A 40 mm mask field is reduced to a 10 mm substrate field by a nominal −0.25× projection relay.

  1. Compare mask and substrate feature scales using the 4:1 reduction.
  2. Keep the substrate at the image conjugate and separate illumination-pupil tuning from magnification.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 365 nm illuminator — Illuminate the mask field uniformly.
  2. 40 mm mask field — Object plane defines pattern geometry.
  3. −0.25× projection (Thin convex lens) — A 200/50 mm relay is one paraxial realization.
  4. 10 mm substrate field (Sample) — Image plane receives the reduced pattern.
  5. Illumination pupil — Controls angular spectrum independently of mask features.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Illuminated reticle or modulator followed by a corrected reduction imaging system.

Key constraints

Resolution, field, distortion, telecentricity, and illumination coherence must be coordinated.
