WAVE-07 · Optical layout · Setup available

Cold-stop matching

Restrict an infrared detector's optical view with a cooled pupil stop.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The setup provides native pupil geometry and clipping. Stop temperature, thermal emission, cryogenic optics and background noise are not modeled; the lens proxy is geometric rather than an infrared material prescription.

The setup

A native 4 µm source illuminates an entrance aperture that is reimaged through two lenses onto a second matched slit. A downstream camera shows admitted and clipped rays.

  1. Compare the entrance and reimaged aperture sizes and positions.
  2. Displace or narrow the second slit and inspect the loss of accepted rays.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Slit
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Relay the system pupil onto a cold aperture in the detector optical train.

Design constraints

Pupil mismatch clips desired light or admits unwanted thermal emission.
