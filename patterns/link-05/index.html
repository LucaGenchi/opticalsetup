The example

Three single-mode inputs map into a three-mode fiber; the reverse lantern separates modes into three coherent receiver channels.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Three SM inputs — Three independent input channels.
  2. Input lantern — Adiabatic merger preserves three guided degrees of freedom.
  3. Three-mode fiber — Transport supports three selected spatial modes.
  4. Output lantern — Reverse transition separates guided modes.
  5. Receiver 1 (Photodetector) — Measure the first output channel.
  6. Receiver 2 (Photodetector) — Measure the second output channel.
  7. Receiver 3 (Photodetector) — Measure the third output channel.
