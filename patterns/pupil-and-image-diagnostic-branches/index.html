CONTROL-08 · Optical layout · Setup available

Pupil and image diagnostic branches

Observe field and aperture planes independently to diagnose an optical train.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native ray profiles and focal geometry are traced. The source pupil is a collimated test beam; this is not a full microscope pupil/image prescription, and no automated diagnosis or wavefront reconstruction is performed.

The setup

Two actual beamsplitters preserve a main beam while providing a collimated-plane camera and a lens/focal-plane camera. The scene makes the two measurement planes physically distinct.

  1. Compare the unfocused diagnostic beam width with the focal-plane spot span.
  2. Move the focusing lens away from its designed distance and inspect the changed focal-plane reading while the direct diagnostic remains unchanged.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Camera
  • Thin convex lens

Arrangement and variants

Switchable or simultaneous cameras/eyepieces conjugated to image and pupil planes.

Design constraints

Probe branches must represent the working path without creating misleading conjugates.
