Patterns / Illuminating and projecting

ILL-06 · Pattern family · Setup available

Light-sheet illumination

Restrict excitation to a thin plane within the observed volume.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native rays demonstrate an orthogonal illumination/detection section. The second transverse axis, actual sheet extent and thickness, diffraction-limited sectioning and volumetric reconstruction are not available in this 2D scene.

The setup

A real focusing meridian illuminates a fluorescent sample from the side while an orthogonal objective and tube lens collect its emitted signal above. The illumination lens represents a cylindrical powered section.

  1. Follow the independent illumination and collection axes.
  2. Move the fluorescence sample away from the illumination focus and inspect geometric overlap.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Sample
  • Objective
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Cylindrical focusing or a scanned beam; orthogonal or oblique detection arrangements.

Design constraints

Sheet thickness, useful propagation length, and access geometry trade against each other.

References

Applications

Combine with

What follows

Fluorescence collection along the detection axis; the illumination path ends beyond the sheet.
