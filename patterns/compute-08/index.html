The example

Two 100 mm lenses form a 4f relay with a circular low-pass aperture at their shared Fourier plane; a clear bypass supplies a comparison image.

  1. Locate all four focal distances in the proposed arrangement.
  2. Compare smoothing with the information removed by suppressing high frequencies.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Transmissive object (Sample) — Place object one focal length before lens 1.
  2. Lens 1: 100 mm (Thin convex lens) — Map angles to the shared Fourier plane.
  3. Fourier aperture (Slit) — Select low spatial frequencies at the common focal plane.
  4. Lens 2: 100 mm (Thin convex lens) — Invert the Fourier transform into the output image.
  5. Filtered image (Camera) — Image plane one focal length after lens 2.
  6. Clear-mask comparison — Repeat acquisition with the aperture removed.
