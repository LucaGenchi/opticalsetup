The example

A collimated probe records transmission through a rotating specimen at 1° increments over 180°; a flat-field frame accompanies the projections.

  1. Distinguish rotation angle from camera pixel position in a sinogram.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Uniform probe (Point source) — Illumination for transmission projections.
  2. Collimator (Thin convex lens) — Approximate parallel-beam acquisition.
  3. Rotating specimen (Sample on piezo stage) — One exposure at each angle from 0° to 179°.
  4. Projection camera (Camera) — Record transmitted intensity.
  5. Sinogram / inverse — Form line integrals after dark and flat-field correction.
  6. Empty-field frame — Measure I0 before inserting the specimen.
