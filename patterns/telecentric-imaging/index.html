IMG-05 · Pattern family · Setup available

Telecentric imaging

Control chief-ray directions in object space, image space, or both.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. A 1D slit is the meridional aperture section; no circular-pupil or unlimited-depth sharpness claim is made.

The setup

A 100 mm lens and a 2 mm stop at its rear focal plane form an object-space telecentric meridional relay. The off-axis emitting point is 200 mm before the lens.

  1. Inspect which object rays pass the rear-focal stop.
  2. Move the stop away from x = 300 mm and compare the admitted chief-ray directions.

Components in this setup

  • Object
  • Thin convex lens
  • Slit
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Place the aperture stop at the appropriate focal plane; object, image, or double telecentricity.

Design constraints

Telecentricity has a finite tolerance and does not remove defocus or diffraction.
