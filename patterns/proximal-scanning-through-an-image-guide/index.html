ACCESS-03 · Optical layout · No setup available

Proximal scanning through an image guide

Place the scanner outside the probe and address its ordered fiber channels.

No setup available

The required coherent image bundle and core-addressed confocal return mapping are absent; scanning a single fiber does not implement proximal image-guide scanning.

Arrangement and variants

Proximal focused scan across an image bundle with distal delivery optics.

Design constraints

Core sampling, coupling, and return-path behaviour constrain field and resolution.

References

  1. Hughes and Yang: fiber-bundle confocal endomicroscopy with descanned detection
  2. SCHOTT: flexible coherent imaging bundles

Used in

Endoscopy and remote probes · Microscopy · Ophthalmic imaging

Applications

Confocal endomicroscopy · forward-looking OCT

Related patterns

Coherent image-bundle transport (ACCESS-02); Pupil-conjugate scanning (SCAN-01); Confocal excitation and detection (CONTRAST-01).

Combine with

Pupil-conjugate scanning (SCAN-01) for proximal addressing; Confocal excitation and detection (CONTRAST-01) for confocal collection.

What follows

Distal illumination followed by a return through the guide to detection.
