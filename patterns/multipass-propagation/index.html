Patterns / Scanning and changing optical paths

SCAN-08 · Pattern family · No setup available

Multipass propagation

Obtain a long interaction path in a compact volume.

No setup available

A true Herriott cell needs the two-transverse-axis off-axis spot orbit and independently placed entrance/exit mirror holes. The 2D mirror model only supplies a central hole and cannot represent the indispensable selected 3D exit spot.

Mirror separation and curvature alone do not specify the 3D Herriott orbit; no gas-line absorption spectrum or spot sequence is calculated.

Arrangement and variants

Herriott or White cells; other geometrically closed sequences of reflections.

Design constraints

Spot separation, mirror apertures, accumulated loss, and injection/extraction govern pass count.

References

  1. Herriott et al.: off-axis paths in spherical-mirror interferometers

Used in

Spectroscopy and analytical chemistry · Ultrafast optics · Chemical and surface sensing

Applications

Gas absorption · nonlinear broadening · compact long-path sensing

Related patterns

Resonant enhancement (CAV-02); Nonlinear broadening plus compression (PULSE-04); Fourier-transform spectroscopy (SPECT-06).

Combine with

Excitation cleanup and rejection (SPECT-07) for absorption sensing; Nonlinear broadening plus compression (PULSE-04) for nonlinear broadening.

What follows

Spectral detection in gas sensing, or Dispersion compensation (PULSE-02) compensation after pulse broadening.
