ROUTE-02 · Optical layout · Setup available

Polarization combining and splitting

Use orthogonal polarization states as separable optical channels.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Ideal Stokes projection is traced; finite PBS extinction, coating phase and depolarization are absent.

The setup

A 532 nm beam with linear polarization at 45° enters a PBS and divides into orthogonal polarization channels.

  1. Compare detector powers and polarization states in the traced example.
  2. Set input polarization to 0° or 90° and verify extinction of the complementary port.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Polarizing BS
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Polarizing beamsplitter with polarization preparation or recovery optics.

Design constraints

Finite extinction and downstream birefringence mix the channels.
