Patterns / Preparing and transforming beams

BEAM-08 · Pattern family · Setup available

Programmable wavefront shaping

Control a field's spatial phase or amplitude through a relayed modulator.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native SLM phase-gradient functions steer and focus rays. The scene does not synthesize a phase hologram, compute coherent order efficiency or validate equal spot irradiance.

The setup

A native transmissive SLM with a programmed two-lenslet layer directs a 24 mm beam into two geometrical focal spots 80 mm downstream.

  1. Inspect the two focal channels and their camera profile.
  2. Change the lens-array layer to three lenslets or adjust focal length; the traced paths update directly.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • SLM
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Spatial light modulator or deformable mirror with relay and order-selection optics.

Design constraints

Modulator sampling, polarization, efficiency, and calibration bound the attainable field.
