ILL-01 · Optical layout

Kohler illumination

Control illuminated field and illumination aperture through separate conjugate-plane sets.

The example

A lamp filament is conjugate to the condenser aperture while the field diaphragm is conjugate to the specimen.

  1. Follow source/aperture and field/specimen conjugate sets independently.
  2. Close the field diaphragm to the observed field and adjust aperture for illumination angle.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Lamp filament (Point source) — Extended source, intentionally not focused on the specimen.
  2. Collector (Thin convex lens) — Images the filament into the aperture plane.
  3. Aperture diaphragm — Sets angular illumination extent.
  4. Condenser (Thin convex lens) — Forms the field-diaphragm image at the specimen.
  5. Specimen field (Sample) — Field and angular controls are independent.
  6. Field diaphragm — Limits illuminated area; focus its rim at the specimen.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Image the source into an aperture plane and the field stop into the specimen plane.

Key constraints

Both conjugate sets must be aligned; uniformity still depends on source and optical quality.
