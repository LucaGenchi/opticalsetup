Homodyne / heterodyne reception

Mix a signal with a local oscillator to read field quadratures or beat signals.

The example

A 1550 nm signal and a local oscillator offset by 10 MHz mix at a 50/50 coupler; balanced electronics isolate a 10 MHz beat.

  1. Follow the strong LO separately from the weak signal.
  2. Compare zero-frequency homodyne reception with the selected 10 MHz heterodyne offset.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 1550 nm signal — Weak measured optical field.
  2. 10 MHz-offset LO (CW Laser) — Coherent local oscillator with chosen frequency offset.
  3. 50/50 optical mixer (Beamsplitter) — Match spatial mode and polarization.
  4. Complementary port A (Photodetector) — One optical mixer output.
  5. Complementary port B (Photodetector) — Opposite beat phase.
  6. 10 MHz balanced beat — Electrical subtraction recovers the beat amplitude and phase.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Mode-matched signal and local oscillator combined before detection.

Key constraints

Relative coherence, polarization, phase noise, and detector bandwidth are essential.
