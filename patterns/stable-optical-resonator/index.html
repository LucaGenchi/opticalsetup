Patterns / Recirculating and amplifying light

CAV-01 · Pattern family · Setup available

Stable optical resonator

Recirculate a supported optical mode through a chosen sequence of surfaces.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This is a passive, externally injected cavity geometry. The renderer shows finite traced passes, not resonant buildup, a Gaussian eigenmode, longitudinal resonance or laser gain.

The setup

An external native 1064 nm source weakly couples through the first of two partially transmitting curved mirrors separated by 300 mm. A leaked-light detector is outside the right mirror.

  1. Inspect mirror curvature and repeated reflections; compute the paraxial g-product separately.
  2. Move the end mirror or offset the seed and compare the traced escape geometry.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Concave mirror
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Linear, ring, and bow-tie geometries with suitable focusing and coupling.

Design constraints

Geometric stability, losses, dispersion, and mode selection must be checked together.
