ROUTE-01 · Pattern family · Setup available

Wavelength combining and splitting

Route different spectral bands through shared or separate optical paths.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The native dichroic model uses ideal wavelength bands; angle-dependent coating spectra, transition width and leakage are absent.

The setup

Spatially separate 488 and 640 nm native lasers are combined by a first 550 nm dichroic. A second dichroic separates their common beam into two wavelength-selective detector ports.

  1. Follow the two physically separate source inputs into the shared axis.
  2. Move the second dichroic cutoff below 488 nm to send both colors to the transmitted detector.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Dichroic mirror
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Dichroic trees, filter cascades, or dispersive separators.

Design constraints

Band edges shift with incidence and polarization; leakage and loss accumulate.
