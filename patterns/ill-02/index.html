ILL-02 · Optical layout

Critical illumination

Image the light source directly onto the plane being illuminated.

Diagram scope: No extended LED image or uniformity metric is computed; the condenser is an illustrative relay rather than a solved source imaging lens. The editable diagram contains annotations and connection arrows; it does not run an optical simulation.

The example

A 1 mm LED die is directly imaged onto a 2 mm specimen field with a nominal 2× condenser relay.

  1. Locate the source image at the specimen.
  2. Compare a structured LED die with a smooth emitting surface and identify the expected illumination texture.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 1 mm LED die (Point source) — Die texture is part of the imaged source.
  2. Condenser relay (Thin convex lens) — Nominal 2× image magnification.
  3. 2 mm specimen field (Sample) — Source nonuniformity can appear here.
  4. Inspection camera (Camera) — Inspect imprint of the die structure.
  5. Die texture — Contrast this source image with Köhler illumination.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Collector optics form a source image at the target.

Key constraints

Source structure appears at the target; useful throughput competes with uniformity.
