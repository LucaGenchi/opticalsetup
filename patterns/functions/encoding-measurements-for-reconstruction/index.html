Ptychographic acquisition

Use overlapping illuminated regions to constrain diffraction-based reconstruction.

COMPUTE-04 · Measurement architecture · No setup available

Coded-aperture imaging

Encode a scene with a known mask and recover it using a measurement model.

The workbench has no 2D binary coded mask and 2D shadowgram detector. Its one-dimensional detector profiles cannot represent the proposed coded-aperture measurements.

COMPUTE-05 · Measurement architecture · No setup available

Single-pixel imaging

Recover spatial information from known patterns and integrated measurements.

The DMD cannot sequence a calibrated 64×64 complementary Hadamard illumination basis, and there is no spatially integrated mask/sample bucket-acquisition model.
