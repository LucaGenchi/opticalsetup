The example

Two separated telescope feeds are delay-matched and combined in K band; a fringe tracker controls delay while a science spectrometer records fringes.

  1. Follow the delay feedback separately from the science output.
  2. Compare a single baseline fringe measurement with complete aperture-synthesis image recovery.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Telescope A — First aperture on a chosen 50 m baseline.
  2. Telescope B — Second aperture observes the same source.
  3. Feed A delay (Mechanical delay line) — Compensates changing geometric path difference.
  4. Feed B relay (Thin convex lens) — Preserves the second aperture field.
  5. K-band combiner (Beamsplitter) — Chosen central wavelength 2.2 µm.
  6. Fringe tracker — Measures residual phase and commands delay correction.
  7. Science spectrometer (Diffraction grating) — Records spectrally dispersed coherent signal.
