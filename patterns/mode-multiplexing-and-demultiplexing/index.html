Mode multiplexing and demultiplexing

Map spatial modes to distinguishable channels and back.

No setup available

The registry has no photonic lantern, spatial-mode-resolved guided fiber or coherent modal receiver. Its drawn fibers do not preserve or separate three spatial eigenmodes.

Arrangement and variants

Photonic lanterns, mode converters, or spatial mode-sorter arrangements.

Design constraints

Supported mode count, crosstalk, and mode-dependent loss constrain the mapping.
