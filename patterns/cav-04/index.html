Master oscillator-power amplifier

Separate beam generation and spectral control from power amplification.

The example

A narrowband 1064 nm master oscillator seeds an isolated amplifier. A pump branch supplies gain energy; a post-amplifier 1% tap monitors output before delivery.

  1. Separate seed coherence from the energy supplied by the pump.
  2. Identify the reverse path the isolator must suppress and the limits of post-amplifier monitoring.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 1064 nm master (CW Laser) — Defines seed frequency and temporal structure.
  2. Input isolator (Optical isolator) — Suppresses amplifier feedback into the oscillator.
  3. Power amplifier — Gain stage is seeded rather than a free-running oscillator.
  4. Pump supply — Adds energy to the gain medium, separate from the seed path.
  5. 1% output tap (Beamsplitter) — Samples amplified light before delivery.
  6. Delivered beam — Output goes to the experiment.
  7. Power monitor (Photodetector) — Separately monitors extraction stability.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Controlled seed source followed by one or more amplifier stages.

Key constraints

Amplified noise, feedback, nonlinear effects, and thermal distortion constrain scaling.
