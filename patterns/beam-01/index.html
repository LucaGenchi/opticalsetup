Spatial filtering and recollimation

Select a cleaner spatial distribution at a focus and recollimate it.

The example

A 532 nm beam is focused by a 20 mm lens through a 10 µm pinhole and recollimated by a 100 mm lens.

  1. Locate the pinhole at the shared focal plane.
  2. Compare pinhole size to the diffraction spot before expecting transmission.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 532 nm laser (CW Laser) — A nominal 2 mm collimated input.
  2. 20 mm focus lens (Thin convex lens) — Focuses near the pinhole.
  3. 10 µm pinhole — Rejects off-axis spatial content at the focus.
  4. 100 mm collimator (Thin convex lens) — Output beam is nominally expanded fivefold.
  5. Clean output — Inspect throughput and alignment trade-offs.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Focusing optic, pinhole or slit, and collimator.

Key constraints

A smaller aperture rejects more structure but loses power and demands better alignment.
