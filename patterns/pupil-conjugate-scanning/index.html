SCAN-01 · Optical layout · Setup available

Pupil-conjugate scanning

Move a focused spot while keeping the scan pivot conjugate to the objective pupil.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. Objective pupils and high-NA field distortion are generic; the setup does not calibrate a commercial scanner.

The setup

A real galvo feeds a 75/75 mm pupil relay and a fixed generic objective. The camera monitors the focused meridional scan.

  1. Change the galvo command by a small mechanical angle and inspect focal displacement.
  2. Move a relay lens and compare the pupil walk and collection.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Galvo mirror
  • Thin convex lens
  • Objective
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Scanner followed by scan/tube-lens relay and focusing objective.

Design constraints

Pupil walk, clipping, aberrations, and scan range determine useful field.
