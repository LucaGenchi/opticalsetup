Multifocal / varifocal presentation

Present images at several focal distances or adjust focal distance dynamically.

A tunable lens cycles through 0, 1 and 2 diopter presentation states while the image engine updates the corresponding depth layer.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Three image layers — Render far, middle and near content separately.
  2. Fast display — Present the matching image during each focus state.
  3. Tunable lens (Thin convex lens) — Target 0, 1 and 2 D optical-power offsets.
  4. Viewer (Human eye) — Observe a time-multiplexed depth presentation.
  5. Focus/image clock — Pair display exposure with lens settling.
  6. Focus calibration — Measure physical lens state before scheduling frames.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Switchable focus, multiple image planes, or synchronized depth-dependent presentation.

Key constraints

Focus range, switching time, registration, and brightness must match displayed content.
