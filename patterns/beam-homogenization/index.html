Patterns / Preparing and transforming beams

BEAM-06 · Pattern family · Setup available

Beam homogenization

Mix or overlap beam portions to reduce illumination nonuniformity.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The arrays are actual separate native lenses in one meridional section. Beamlet paths and overlap are computed; coherent speckle, 2D fly-eye uniformity and microlens diffraction are not.

The setup

Two real three-lens arrays form three meridional beamlet channels, followed by a 100 mm field lens. The camera receives their overlapping geometrical contributions.

  1. Follow each lenslet channel through both arrays and the field lens.
  2. Move one second-array lenslet transversely and inspect loss of common overlap.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Fly-eye microlens arrays with a condenser; integrating rods or light pipes.

Design constraints

Uniformity trades against angular spread, losses, and coherence-related speckle.
