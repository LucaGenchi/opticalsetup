Serial image relay

Transfer an image through a long narrow optical channel.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Paraxial 2D object rays and relay imaging are shown. This is a lens-built serial relay, not a commercial rod-lens prescription or a calibrated rigid-endoscope aberration model.

The setup

A native luminous object at 100 mm feeds two 4f relays built from four 50 mm thin lenses. The intermediate image lies at 300 mm and the final camera at 500 mm.

  1. Inspect the real rays and identify object, intermediate and final image planes.
  2. Move the final camera away from the conjugate plane or change one focal length and compare image spread.

Components in this setup

  • Object
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Repeated relay stages using rod lenses, conventional lenses, or GRIN elements.

Design constraints

Diameter, field, numerical aperture, accumulated aberrations, and assembly tolerances compete.
