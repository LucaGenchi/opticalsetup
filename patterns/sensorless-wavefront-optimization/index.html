Patterns / Correcting wavefronts and rejecting background

WAVE-03 · Control architecture · Setup available

Sensorless wavefront optimization

Optimize an optical quality metric instead of using a dedicated wavefront sensor.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The scene supports manual image-metric adjustment of native tip/tilt and defocus. It has no automated coefficient search, higher-order wavefront correction or sample-dependent optimization guarantee.

The setup

A native deformable mirror sends a finite-width beam to a camera. Its focal-length control changes paraxial convergence; the camera spot provides the metric without a separate wavefront sensor.

  1. Sweep DM defocus focal length and compare the measured spot profile at the fixed camera.
  2. Move the camera plane and repeat the adjustment; an optimum for one plane is not automatically an optimum elsewhere.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Deformable mirror
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Programmable corrector, measured image or signal metric, and optimization loop.

Design constraints

Metric choice, sample changes, and measurement count affect convergence and interpretability.
