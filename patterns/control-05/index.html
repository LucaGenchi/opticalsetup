The example

An 850 nm auxiliary beam reflects from a coverslip through the imaging objective; a split detector drives sample z while a visible camera records the specimen.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 850 nm probe (CW Laser) — Auxiliary wavelength separated from visible imaging.
  2. Auxiliary dichroic (Dichroic mirror) — Combine IR focus beam with imaging path.
  3. Imaging objective (Objective) — Deliver focus probe and collect visible sample light.
  4. Coverslip / sample (Sample) — Reflect IR from a stable interface.
  5. Z actuator (Sample on piezo stage) — Move objective or sample to correct error.
  6. Split focus detector (Photodetector) — Off-axis return displacement supplies a signed error.
  7. Focus servo — Calibrate setpoint near the intended imaging focus.
  8. Visible camera (Camera) — Independent scientific image output.
