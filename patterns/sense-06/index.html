SENSE-06 · Measurement architecture

Crossed-beam Doppler measurement

Convert particle motion through an interference region into a measurable modulation.

The example

Two 532 nm beams cross at a 20° full angle in a seeded flow; scattered modulation measures velocity across approximately 1.53 µm fringes.

  1. Calculate fringe spacing from wavelength and crossing angle.
  2. Explain the directional ambiguity without a frequency-shifted arm.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 532 nm source (CW Laser) — Shared coherent input.
  2. Beam splitter (Beamsplitter) — Feed matched illumination arms.
  3. Upper crossing arm (Mirror) — Steer +10° toward the probe volume.
  4. Flow probe volume — Velocity component normal to fringes is measured.
  5. Scattered receiver (Photodetector) — Collect particle-scattered light.
  6. Lower crossing arm (Mirror) — Steer −10° toward the same volume.
  7. Frequency counter — Use v=fD λ/(2 sin10°).

Arrangement and variants

Fringe geometry, particle scattering, and detection bandwidth set measurement range.
