Patterns / Comparing optical fields

FIELD-06 · Pattern family · Setup available

Homodyne / heterodyne reception

Mix a signal with a local oscillator to read field quadratures or beat signals.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The optical heterodyne mixing layout and branch powers are traced. Static camera readout does not simulate a 10 MHz temporal beat, balanced RF receiver or coherent phase locking of independent lasers.

The setup

Two real native laser sources enter orthogonal ports of a 50/50 optical coupler. A nominal 10 MHz optical-frequency offset is encoded by a roughly 0.00008014 nm wavelength separation near 1550 nm.

  1. Inspect both source contributions at the output ports.
  2. Block either source and compare collected optical power.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Mode-matched signal and local oscillator combined before detection.

Design constraints

Relative coherence, polarization, phase noise, and detector bandwidth are essential.
