ILL-04 · Pattern family · Setup available

Pupil-shaped illumination

Choose the angular illumination distribution by structuring a pupil plane.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This is an actual 2D cross-section of annular illumination. A complete azimuthal cone, scattering contrast and dark-field specimen response require 3D scattering and are not calculated.

The setup

Real opaque masks admit the two meridional sections of an annular pupil, between 9 and 12 mm from the axis. A condenser brings the oblique ray bundles to the specimen camera.

  1. Follow the blocked central beam and the two surviving pupil bands.
  2. Increase the central obscuration and compare transmitted power.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beam dump
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Pupil masks, source shaping, or a modulator relayed to an illumination pupil.

Design constraints

Field uniformity, angular coverage, coherence, and throughput must be designed together.

References

Applications

Annular illumination · dipole or quadrupole illumination · contrast control

Combine with

What follows
