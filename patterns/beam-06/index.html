BEAM-06 · Pattern family

Beam homogenization

Mix or overlap beam portions to reduce illumination nonuniformity.

Schematic exampleFly-eye homogenizer

Uneven input — Nominal 6 mm illumination beam.

The example

Two 300 µm-pitch microlens arrays and a 100 mm field lens overlap beamlets at a 5 mm target field.

  1. Trace both a representative beamlet and the combined target field.
  2. Distinguish averaging irradiance from eliminating coherent speckle.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Uneven input (CW Laser) — Nominal 6 mm illumination beam.
  2. First lenslet array — Partitions the aperture into beamlets.
  3. Second lenslet array — Relays each beamlet toward a common field lens.
  4. 100 mm field lens (Thin convex lens) — Superposes many lenslet images.
  5. 5 mm target field — Beamlet overlap reduces sensitivity to input structure.
  6. Representative beamlet — One channel among many; not an extra source.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Fly-eye microlens arrays with a condenser; integrating rods or light pipes.

Key constraints

Uniformity trades against angular spread, losses, and coherence-related speckle.
