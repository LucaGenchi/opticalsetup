COMPUTE-06 · Measurement architecture

Light-field capture

Sample ray position and direction to support view or focus reconstruction.

The example

A 100 µm-pitch microlens array at the main image plane maps local ray angles onto camera subimages; a calibration frame determines lenslet centers.

  1. Identify which coordinates represent position and which represent angle.
  2. Explain why allocating more pixels per lenslet reduces spatial sampling.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Three-depth scene — Objects at three distances provide refocus targets.
  2. Main imaging lens (Thin convex lens) — Form an image at the microlens array.
  3. 100 µm lenslets — Each lenslet samples one spatial neighborhood.
  4. Subimage sensor (Camera) — Placed near a 2 mm lenslet focal plane.
  5. Digital refocus — Combine calibrated angular samples.
  6. Lenslet calibration — Uniform-field image locates subimage centers.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Microlens array near an image plane or equivalent angular sampling optics.

Key constraints

Finite sensor sampling trades spatial resolution against angular information.
