Image rotation and derotation

The example

Three plane mirrors on one rotating mount derotate an astronomical field; a 15° mechanical rotation corresponds to a nominal 30° image rotation.

  1. Follow the three reflections rather than interpreting the folded path as an image rotation simulation.
  2. Compare the nominal twice-angle image rotation with the mount angle.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Rotating sky field — A field whose orientation changes while tracking.
  2. First fold (Mirror) — First reflector on the shared rotary mount.
  3. Central mirror (Mirror) — Second reflection defines the folded K path.
  4. Third fold (Mirror) — Third reflection returns toward the output direction.
  5. Derotated camera (Camera) — Orientation is controlled by rotating the assembly.
  6. 15° mount rotation — All three mirrors rotate together.
