The example

Two symmetric nonimaging walls funnel rays within a 30° half-angle toward a small detector; an off-angle channel identifies rejected input.

  1. Compare imaging requirements with power collection requirements.
  2. Use etendue conservation to bound concentration before refining geometry.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Accepted angular fan — Incident rays within ±30° of axis.
  2. Upper CPC wall — First reflective boundary.
  3. Lower CPC wall — Second reflective boundary.
  4. Small exit aperture (Slit) — Concentration target rather than image plane.
  5. Power receiver (Photodetector) — Collect accepted output.
  6. Outside acceptance — Example +45° input should not be assumed collected.
