Nulling interferometry

Suppress a dominant coherent component through controlled destructive interference.

The example

Two equal-amplitude aperture feeds at 3.8 µm are combined with a π relative phase to reject an on-axis coherent source at the science port.

  1. Compare the π phase bias with equal optical path before the bias.
  2. Introduce amplitude or phase imbalance conceptually and identify why the dark port leaks.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Aperture A — One collector of the on-axis source.
  2. Aperture B — Second equal-amplitude collector.
  3. Path equalizer (Mechanical delay line) — Match optical path before phase bias.
  4. π phase bias (Phase object) — At 3.8 µm, π corresponds to 1.9 µm optical path.
  5. Nulling combiner (Beamsplitter) — Matched amplitude, polarization and spatial modes.
  6. Dark science port (Photodetector) — On-axis coherent field is nominally suppressed.
  7. Bright monitor port (Photodetector) — Complementary port tracks total throughput.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Phase-controlled recombination with matched amplitudes and modes.

Key constraints

Small phase, polarization, or amplitude errors leave residual leakage.
