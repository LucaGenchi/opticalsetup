BEAM-04 · Pattern family · Setup available

Anamorphic beam shaping

Adjust the two transverse beam axes independently.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The native ideal lenses represent the powered cross-section of cylindrical optics. The unpowered transverse axis, astigmatism and a complete 2D beam cross-section are not simulated.

The setup

The powered meridian of a 25/100 mm cylindrical telescope is represented by native ideal lenses separated by 125 mm. Rays expand from 2 to 8 mm along the modeled axis.

  1. Compare input and output width in the powered meridional section.
  2. Change lens separation to see residual convergence; the orthogonal unpowered axis is outside this 2D scene.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Cylindrical telescopes, anamorphic prism pairs, or freeform optics.

Design constraints

Astigmatism, orientation, and unequal axis magnifications require coordinated design.
