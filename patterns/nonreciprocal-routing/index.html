Patterns / Separating, combining, and routing channels

ROUTE-05 · Pattern family · Setup available

Nonreciprocal routing

Direct light differently in forward and reverse propagation.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The registry isolator is an ideal directional gate, not a resolved Faraday-rotator/polarizer assembly. Magneto-optic dispersion, isolation ratios and damage thresholds are not computed.

The setup

Two native test rows demonstrate the isolator directional gate: the forward source reaches its camera, while an independent backward source is stopped.

  1. Compare the forward camera with the blocked reverse test row.
  2. Reverse the isolator orientation and inspect which test direction is admitted.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Optical isolator
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Faraday rotation with polarizers or multiport polarization-routing stages.

Design constraints

Isolation, insertion loss, bandwidth, and power handling constrain operation.
