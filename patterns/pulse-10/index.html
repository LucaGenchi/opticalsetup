The example

A synchronized near-infrared gate traverses a thin electro-optic crystal with a THz field. A quarter-wave bias and polarization splitter feed balanced photodiodes while gate delay samples the field.

  1. Trace the THz interaction separately from the optical gate detection.
  2. Compare crystal thickness with temporal response; a stronger signal can sacrifice measurement bandwidth.

Follow the arrangement

  1. THz field — The unknown electric field induces birefringence.
  2. Delayed optical gate (Pulsed Laser) — A short gate samples successive THz arrival times.
  3. Electro-optic crystal (Crystal) — Velocity matching and thickness govern temporal bandwidth.
  4. Quarter-wave bias (λ/4 waveplate) — Sets a sensitive polarization working point.
  5. Polarization analyzer (Polarizing BS) — Converts small retardance into opposite intensity changes.
  6. Photodiode + (Photodetector) — One analyzer output.
  7. Photodiode − (Photodetector) — The complementary output enables differential readout.
