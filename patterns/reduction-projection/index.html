ILL-07 · Optical layout · Setup available

Reduction projection

Transfer a mask or modulator image to a target at reduced scale.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. This is the projection subsystem; no digital spatial modulator or calibrated image brightness is simulated.

The setup

A real 200/50 mm relay projects a native object with quarter-scale ideal magnification onto the camera. The long first focal length and shorter second focal length create the reduction.

  1. Compare the paraxial object marker size with the original 10 mm object.
  2. Change the camera position and inspect the loss of conjugacy.

Components in this setup

  • Object
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Illuminated reticle or modulator followed by a corrected reduction imaging system.

Design constraints

Resolution, field, distortion, telecentricity, and illumination coherence must be coordinated.
