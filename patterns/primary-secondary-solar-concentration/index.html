DISPLAY-07 · Pattern family · Setup available

Primary-secondary solar concentration

Use a main collector and a local second stage to deliver sunlight to a receiver.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This native ray setup is a small reflective solar-collection geometry with a folding secondary, not a CPC secondary or thermal plant. The laboratory test laser is not a calibrated extended solar-radiance source; no 3D flux, thermal efficiency, tracking control or spectral throughput is calculated.

The setup

One collimated 550 nm test beam probes this 2D solar-collector geometry. Source angles can be changed manually to sample the solar disk’s approximately ±0.25° range. A 70 mm-focal-length parabolic primary and small plane secondary direct collected light onto an offset receiver.

  1. Follow actual reflected rays from the primary through the secondary to the receiver; inspect the receiver spot and central obstruction.
  2. Move the secondary mirror out of the beam to remove the focused receive path (small unfocused stray input can remain), or widen the incident angular spread to inspect off-axis collection loss.

Arrangement and variants

Primary dish, trough, Fresnel, or mirror field with suitable secondary optics.

Tracking, acceptance, receiver flux uniformity, and thermal loads determine usefulness.
