The example

A blue LED injects into a rectangular guide; three progressively stronger extraction zones illuminate a diffuser while a terminal reflector returns residual power.

  1. Compare extraction density with remaining guided power.
  2. Identify the extra transport calculation needed to predict uniform brightness.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 450 nm edge source (Point source) — Proposed blue source along one panel edge.
  2. Input mixer (Diffuser) — Reduce input hot spots before guide distribution.
  3. Sparse extraction — Low extraction near the illuminated edge.
  4. Medium extraction — Higher density farther from the source.
  5. Dense extraction — Strong extraction near the remote edge.
  6. Output diffuser (Diffuser) — Blend spatial extraction into panel illumination.
  7. End reflector (Mirror) — Recycle residual guided power.
