Patterns / Separating and measuring spectra

SPECT-01 · Pattern family · Setup available

Slit-collimator-disperser-camera

Map wavelength to detector position while imaging an entrance slit.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native slit clipping and grating diffraction are traced in 2D. This compact transmission layout does not compute a calibrated spectral resolving power, blaze response or optical aberration budget.

The setup

A native monochromatic source illuminates a 2 mm entrance slit, a 100 mm collimator, a diffraction grating and a camera lens. Change source wavelength to see the traced diffraction angle change.

  1. Inspect the grating order and groove density, then compare diffraction angles at 532 nm and 633 nm.
  2. Change slit gap and watch clipping; detector position must follow the selected diffracted order.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Slit
  • Thin convex lens
  • Diffraction grating
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Czerny-Turner, Littrow, or related dispersive spectrograph layouts.

Design constraints

Slit width, dispersion, aberrations, and detector sampling trade resolution against throughput.
