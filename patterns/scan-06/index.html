SCAN-06 · Pattern family

Fixed-output variable delay

Change optical path length while preserving a defined output geometry.

The example

A retroreflector translates 15 mm on a carriage, changing the free-space round-trip path by 30 mm, approximately 100 ps, with a fixed output axis.

  1. Use Δt = 2Δx/c to convert 15 mm carriage travel to roughly 100 ps.
  2. Check fixed output pointing independently of path-length change.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Fixed input beam (Pulsed Laser) — Input stays aligned during carriage travel.
  2. Input/output fold (Mirror) — Separates parallel in/out paths.
  3. Moving retroreflector (Retroreflector) — Carriage stroke is 15 mm.
  4. Fixed output — Returned beam remains on the output axis.
  5. 15 mm translation (Sample on piezo stage) — Round trip gives twice the carriage displacement.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Translated retroreflector, folded mirror pair, or other compensated delay arrangement.

Key constraints

Travel, beam walk, dispersion, and stability determine usable delay range.
