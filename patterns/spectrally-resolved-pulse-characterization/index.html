Patterns / Handling pulses and nonlinear interactions

PULSE-08 · Pattern family · Setup available

Spectrally resolved pulse characterization

Encode temporal information into spectral measurements for pulse reconstruction.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The saved setup supplies a real nonlinear gate and spectral readout. It does not acquire a FROG delay–wavelength matrix automatically or perform spectral-phase reconstruction; this is a cross-gating implementation, not a completed FROG measurement.

The setup

A native two-color gated measurement combines 1032 nm and 790 nm pulses in a nonlinear crystal and resolves the gated sum-frequency channel on a spectrometer while optical delay is varied.

  1. Scan delay and observe which spectral channel follows the two-pulse overlap.
  2. Compare the spectrum at time zero with one taken outside overlap before attempting any pulse retrieval.

Components in this setup

  • Pulsed Laser
  • Mechanical delay line
  • Mirror
  • Dichroic mirror
  • Thin convex lens
  • Crystal
  • Beam dump
  • Spectrometer
  • Detector screen

Arrangement and variants

FROG uses a spectrally resolved gate; SPIDER uses spectral-shear interferometry.

Design constraints

Each realization has its own sampling, calibration, and retrieval assumptions.
