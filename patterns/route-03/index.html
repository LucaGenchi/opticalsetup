ROUTE-03 · Optical layout

Waveplate-polarizer attenuation

Adjust delivered power by rotating polarization before selecting a channel.

Ray exampleHalf-wave power attenuator

Horizontal input — Linear polarization at 0°.

Open in full canvas ↗Download setup

Ray model: Ideal retardance and Malus-law attenuation are traced; absorption heating and wavelength-dependent plate errors are absent.

The example

A half-wave plate at 22.5° rotates a horizontal 532 nm input to 45°; a horizontal analyzer passes half the input power.

  1. Read half-power transmission at 22.5° in the live scene.
  2. Rotate the HWP to 0° and 45° to compare full transmission and extinction.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Horizontal input (CW Laser) — Linear polarization at 0°.
  2. HWP · 22.5° (λ/2 waveplate) — Output polarization rotates by twice the fast-axis angle.
  3. 0° analyzer (Polarizer) — Malus-law transmission is cos²(45°) = 0.5.
  4. Half-power output (Photodetector) — Read power after the analyzer.
  5. Rejected polarization — Absorbed by the analyzer; not an extra transmitted beam.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Half-wave plate followed by a polarizer or polarizing beamsplitter.

Key constraints

Input polarization, wavelength, extinction, and rejected-power handling matter.
