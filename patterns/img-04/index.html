IMG-04 · Optical layout

Infinity-corrected imaging

Use an objective and tube lens with an intermediate collimated space.

Schematic exampleInfinity microscope with filter space

Specimen — Place specimen at objective front focal plane.

The example

A nominal 10× objective with 20 mm effective focal length and a 200 mm tube lens leaves a collimated region for a 550 nm filter.

  1. Identify where a filter can be inserted without deliberately moving the image plane.
  2. Change tube-lens focal length and calculate the resulting nominal magnification.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Specimen (Sample) — Place specimen at objective front focal plane.
  2. 10× objective (Objective) — Nominal f = 20 mm with a 200 mm tube lens.
  3. 550 nm filter (Filter) — Inserted in infinity space, away from an image conjugate.
  4. 200 mm tube lens (Thin convex lens) — Forms the intermediate image.
  5. Camera image (Camera) — Nominal magnification is −200/20 = −10.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Infinity objective followed by a matched tube lens and detector.

Key constraints

Tube-lens choice, pupil position, and accessory apertures affect magnification and field.
