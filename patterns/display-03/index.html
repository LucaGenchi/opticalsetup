Polarization-folded imaging

The example

Display light enters a partial-reflector cavity; repeated quarter-wave conversion and a reflective polarizer select the eventual output toward the eye.

  1. Enumerate the passes instead of treating the cavity as a single lens.
  2. Identify the polarization condition needed for final transmission.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Polarized display — Prepare the required input polarization.
  2. Partial reflector (Beamsplitter) — Transmit entry light and reflect a later pass.
  3. Retarder (λ/4 waveplate) — Quarter-wave polarization conversion per traversal.
  4. Reflective polarizer (Polarizing BS) — Reflect the first state; transmit the final state.
  5. Eye pupil (Human eye) — Output after the selected folded path.
  6. Folded imaging lens (Thin convex lens) — Supply optical power within the compact path.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Retarders, polarization-selective reflectors, and powered surfaces in a folded path.

Key constraints

Throughput, polarization leakage, ghosts, and broadband retardance constrain quality.
