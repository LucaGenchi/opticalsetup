Patterns / Correcting wavefronts and rejecting background

WAVE-04 · Pattern family · Setup available

Guide-star wavefront correction

Use a localized reference to estimate propagation distortions.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The native bench constructs shared correction and spectral guide/science separation. It does not simulate a sodium beacon, finite-altitude cone effect, absolute tip/tilt recovery or atmospheric wavefront sensing.

The setup

A real 589 nm guide source and 800 nm science source are combined onto a native DM, then separated by wavelength to two cameras. The common correction can be adjusted against the guide image.

  1. Follow both wavelengths through the same corrector and identify their separate sensing/readout cameras.
  2. Adjust DM steering and compare movement of both camera profiles.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Mirror
  • Dichroic mirror
  • Deformable mirror
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Natural or artificial reference, wavefront readout, and corrective optical path.

Design constraints

Reference geometry and target separation determine which aberrations are shared.
