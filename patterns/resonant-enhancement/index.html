CAV-02 · Optical layout · Setup available

Resonant enhancement

Build circulating power from an externally driven coherent input.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The setup constructs the cavity path and local conversion, but does not calculate resonant enhancement, impedance matching, locking, cavity stability or circulating steady-state power.

The setup

A native splitter injects 1064 nm light into a closed four-corner optical path containing an SHG crystal. The return port and conversion are traced through real surfaces.

  1. Follow the closed optical round trip and distinguish crystal conversion from resonance enhancement.
  2. Change a cavity mirror angle or crystal conversion share and inspect escaping versus converted rays.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Mirror
  • Crystal
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Mode-matched source, input coupler, resonator, and resonance control.

Design constraints

Coupling must account for internal losses; detuning and mode mismatch reduce buildup.
