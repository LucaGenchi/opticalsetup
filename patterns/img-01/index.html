IMG-01 · Pattern family

Image relay / 4f relay

Reimage an object plane at a chosen location and magnification.

Ray exampleUnit-magnification 4f relay

Object plane — Place a small target 100 mm before L1.

Open in full canvas ↗Download setup

Ray model: The fan traces on-axis geometrical rays, while the F marker is a separate paraxial image construction that does not model downstream clipping. No target-image irradiance, diffraction-limited transfer function or real-lens aberration is computed.

The example

Two 100 mm thin lenses relay an object plane to an inverted image over 400 mm; the shared focal plane remains accessible.

  1. Open the live scene: the separately drawn ideal image of the 10 mm F is inverted at −1 magnification; the traced fan comes from the on-axis object anchor.
  2. Move the camera 30 mm beyond its image conjugate: the narrow fan broadens and the camera no longer lies at the ideal image plane.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Object plane — Place a small target 100 mm before L1.
  2. L1 · 100 mm (Thin convex lens) — First Fourier transform; object at its front focus.
  3. Fourier plane — 100 mm after L1 and before L2; a spatial-frequency plane.
  4. L2 · 100 mm (Thin convex lens) — Reimages the first plane with magnification −1.
  5. Image plane (Camera) — 100 mm after L2; object features are inverted.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Two-lens focal-plane relay; equal or unequal focal lengths; reflective equivalents.

Key constraints

Conjugate distances, apertures, and aberrations set usable field and resolution.

What follows
