Patterns / Preparing quantum states and manipulating particles

QUANT-04 · Pattern family · No setup available

Time-bin preparation and analysis

Encode and analyze alternatives associated with distinct photon arrival times.

No setup available

There is no single-photon time-bin state preparation, matched quantum analyzer or gated central-slot coincidence counter. Classical delayed pulses cannot implement the specified quantum state.

Arrangement and variants

Matched unbalanced interferometers with phase control and time-resolved detection.

Design constraints

Path imbalance, coherence requirements, and timing resolution depend on protocol.

References

Applications

Time-bin communication · Franson-type measurements

Combine with
