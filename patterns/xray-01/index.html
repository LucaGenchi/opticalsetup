XRAY-01 · Optical layout

Orthogonal grazing-incidence focusing

Focus the two transverse axes using separate crossed grazing-incidence mirrors.

Schematic exampleSequential orthogonal KB line foci

8 keV input — Energy selection precedes the focusing pair.

The example

An illustrative 8 keV beam reflects from a vertically focusing grazing-incidence mirror and then a horizontally focusing mirror. Their separate line-focus powers combine at one sample point.

  1. Identify which mirror controls each transverse dimension.
  2. Move one longitudinal focus conceptually and distinguish an astigmatic pair from a coincident point focus.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 8 keV input — Energy selection precedes the focusing pair.
  2. Vertical KB mirror — A grazing-incidence figure focuses one transverse dimension.
  3. Horizontal KB mirror — The orthogonal mirror focuses the other dimension.
  4. Shared sample focus (Sample) — Both line foci must coincide longitudinally.
  5. Downstream monitor (Photodetector) — Measures transmitted or diffracted light after the specimen.
  6. Separate line-focus check — Alignment checks each focusing dimension independently.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Kirkpatrick-Baez mirror pair with axis-specific figures and alignment.

Key constraints

Grazing geometry, surface error, aperture, and vibration constrain spot size and acceptance.
