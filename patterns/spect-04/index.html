The example

Choose an 8 keV Si(111) teaching beamline: linked crystal angles select energy while translation of the second crystal maintains a fixed-height exit.

  1. Identify the angle and translation degrees of freedom separately.
  2. Scan energy conceptually while holding the exit node fixed; explain why rotation alone is insufficient.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Broad X-ray beam — Incident beam direction is fixed during an energy scan.
  2. First Si(111) crystal — Bragg reflection selects the chosen energy.
  3. Translated second crystal — Linked angle and translation restore the exit axis.
  4. Fixed-height exit — Downstream optics see a stationary nominal beam line.
  5. Downstream focus — Receives the monochromatic output.
  6. Angle / translation law — Motion must follow energy and fixed-exit geometry.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Coordinated double-crystal or dispersive-element motions with output compensation.

Key constraints

Exit stability, spectral purity, throughput, and mechanical tolerances constrain tuning.
