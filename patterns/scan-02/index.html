SCAN-02 · Optical layout

Relay between scan axes

Make separated scanner axes optically conjugate.

Schematic exampleRelayed X and Y galvos

X galvo — First scan axis, at the first conjugate pivot.

The example

A 75/75 mm 4f relay images the X-galvo pivot onto the Y-galvo pivot before a second relay to the objective pupil.

  1. Compare relayed galvos with two nearby but nonconjugate mirrors.
  2. Check that both pivots map to the objective pupil before discussing a 2D raster.

Follow the arrangement

  1. X galvo (Galvo mirror) — First scan axis, at the first conjugate pivot.
  2. 75/75 mm relay (Thin convex lens) — 150 mm lens separation preserves the pivot plane.
  3. Y galvo (Galvo mirror) — Second axis shares a conjugate pupil with the first.
  4. Objective pupil relay (Thin convex lens) — Relays both angular controls to the final pupil.
  5. Two-axis scan field — Objective follows the relay; X and Y are independent coordinates.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Image the first scanning mirror onto the second with an intervening relay.

Key constraints

Relay magnification, mirror apertures, and alignment couple the two scan ranges.
