QUANT-05 · Optical layout · No setup available

Squeezed-field injection and readout

Deliver a squeezed field with the quadrature alignment needed for a measurement.

No setup available

The OPO element does not produce squeezed vacuum or quantum quadrature noise. No shot-noise-referenced homodyne variance receiver exists, so ordinary detector powers cannot represent squeezing.

Arrangement and variants

Squeezed source, mode matching, phase control, and quadrature-sensitive readout.

Design constraints

Loss and phase errors degrade squeezing; useful quadrature can depend on frequency.

References

  1. Squeezed states from an optical parametric oscillator — Wu et al.

Used in

Quantum optics and information · Optical metrology · Astronomy and astrophysics

Applications

Quantum-noise reduction · precision interferometry

Related patterns

Homodyne / heterodyne reception (FIELD-06); Nulling interferometry (FIELD-09); Power and signal recycling (CAV-03).

Combine with

Mode matching (BEAM-03) for mode matching; Homodyne / heterodyne reception (FIELD-06) for quadrature readout.

What follows

The sensing interferometer or homodyne detector selected for the squeezed quadrature.
