Patterns / Scanning and changing optical paths

SCAN-06 · Pattern family · Setup available

Fixed-output variable delay

Change optical path length while preserving a defined output geometry.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native retroreflection and pulsed propagation are traced. Carriage mechanics, air-index dispersion and stage accuracy are not simulated.

The setup

A native pulsed beam enters a real retroreflector 20 mm above its center and returns on a separate fixed axis 20 mm below. Translating the retroreflector changes the round-trip length.

  1. Inspect the distinct input and return axes.
  2. Translate the retroreflector 15 mm right: free-space propagation gains 30 mm, about 100 ps.

Components in this setup

  • Pulsed Laser
  • Retroreflector
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Translated retroreflector, folded mirror pair, or other compensated delay arrangement.

Design constraints

Travel, beam walk, dispersion, and stability determine usable delay range.
