Sample/reference splitting

Provide a simultaneous reference channel alongside the sample channel.

The example

A 532 nm beam sends 90% through a sample with 50% excitation transmission and 10% to an independent reference photodiode.

  1. Compare sample/reference power ratio, nominally 4.5 for the chosen transmissions.
  2. Change source power and verify that both readings scale while their ratio stays fixed.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 532 nm input (CW Laser) — Common source-power fluctuations affect both branches.
  2. 90/10 splitter (Beamsplitter) — 90% transmission and 10% reflection.
  3. 50% sample (Sample) — Sample branch loses half its incident power.
  4. Sample detector (Photodetector) — Expected 45% of source power in this ideal model.
  5. Reference diode (Photodetector) — Expected 10% of source power.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Beam splitter or pickoff with separately characterized sample and reference paths.

Key constraints

Differential losses and detector response can masquerade as sample changes.
