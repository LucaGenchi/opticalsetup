The example

A phase SLM at 532 nm encodes two equal target spots; a blazed carrier separates the selected order from undiffracted light.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 532 nm expanded beam (CW Laser) — Polarization and aperture must suit the SLM.
  2. Phase hologram (SLM) — Adds a carrier and a two-spot phase pattern.
  3. Order-selection plane — Stop selects the intended diffraction order.
  4. Target spot A — One of two equal nominal target powers.
  5. Target spot B — Second programmed target.
  6. Zero-order dump (Beam dump) — Unmodulated light is rejected.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Spatial light modulator or deformable mirror with relay and order-selection optics.

Key constraints

Modulator sampling, polarization, efficiency, and calibration bound the attainable field.
