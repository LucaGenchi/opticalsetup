Polarization diversity

Process both orthogonal polarization components in parallel.

The example

A PBS splits an unknown 1550 nm signal into H and V components; each receives its own coherent hybrid and balanced readout.

  1. Follow both polarization channels instead of discarding one PBS port.
  2. Separate optical polarization diversity from downstream digital polarization tracking.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 1550 nm signal — Unknown polarization may vary in time.
  2. Polarization demux (Polarizing BS) — Keep both orthogonal components.
  3. H coherent hybrid — Mix H signal with a matched local-oscillator component.
  4. V coherent hybrid — Mix V signal with its own LO component.
  5. H I/Q readout (Photodetector) — Calibrated quadratures for H channel.
  6. V I/Q readout (Photodetector) — Calibrated quadratures for V channel.
  7. Shared local oscillator (CW Laser) — Distribute coherent LO light to both hybrids.
