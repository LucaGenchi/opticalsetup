The example

A lensed fiber focuses a 1550 nm input onto an inverse-taper chip edge; a chip splitter provides an alignment witness.

  1. Use the witness port to separate coupling alignment from device response.
  2. Explain why moving a focusing lens cannot prove modal overlap.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Lensed fiber — Example 2.5 µm waist at the facet.
  2. Chip facet — Align transverse position and axial waist.
  3. Inverse taper — Expand the chip mode toward the fiber mode.
  4. On-chip splitter — Create a diagnostic branch after successful coupling.
  5. Device circuit — Main guided output.
  6. Witness detector (Photodetector) — Alignment monitor on a separate port.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Edge coupling with mode converters; grating coupling with focusing and alignment optics.

Key constraints

Mode mismatch, alignment tolerance, polarization, and bandwidth limit coupling.
