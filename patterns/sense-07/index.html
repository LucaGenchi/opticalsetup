Follow the arrangement

  1. 488 nm excitation (CW Laser) — Excite labeled flowing cells.
  2. Flow cell (Sample) — Particles cross the focused illumination.
  3. Forward scatter (Photodetector) — Small-angle channel with central-beam rejection.
  4. 90° collection (Objective) — Collect side scatter and fluorescence.
  5. 560 nm split (Dichroic mirror) — Separate green and red bands.
  6. 530 nm channel (Photodetector) — Example 530/30 nm filter band.
  7. 610 nm channel (Photodetector) — Example 610/20 nm filter band.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Focused excitation with forward/side collection and filtered detector branches.

Key constraints

Collection geometry and spectral overlap require calibration; observables are sample-dependent.
