Virtual-image combiner

Present an optical image while preserving a view of the surrounding scene.

The example

A microdisplay sits at a 25 mm collimator focus; a partial reflector overlays its nominal infinity-focused image on the external scene.

  1. Follow display and real-world light separately into the pupil.
  2. Move the proposed display away from the focus to change virtual-image distance.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Microdisplay — Image source at the collimator focal plane.
  2. 25 mm collimator (Thin convex lens) — Present approximately collimated image bundles.
  3. See-through combiner (Beamsplitter) — Reflect display light while passing the outside world.
  4. Observer pupil (Human eye) — Receive both channels at the eye position.
  5. Virtual infinity image — Perceived direction belongs to collimated display bundles.
  6. External scene — Independent real-world light path.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Partially reflective, freeform, birdbath, or waveguide-based combining arrangements.

Key constraints

Eyebox, field of view, brightness, distortion, and world-view quality trade off.
