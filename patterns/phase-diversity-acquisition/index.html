Patterns / Encoding measurements for reconstruction

COMPUTE-07 · Measurement architecture · Setup available

Phase-diversity acquisition

Use images with known optical differences to estimate image and wavefront jointly.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The native tracer calculates paraxial focusing and detector spot size. The source is a test beam rather than an extended unknown object; no diffraction PSF, aberration estimator, phase-diversity inverse solver or calibrated pupil phase is computed.

The setup

A 6 mm collimated beam is split into two equal branches. Matched 50 mm lenses focus onto one camera at the nominal focal plane and another 10 mm beyond it.

  1. Compare camera spot spans in the focus and defocus branches, then move the defocus camera back to the nominal focus.
  2. Move the defocus camera farther away to increase geometric blur; this is an acquisition geometry rather than a wavefront reconstruction.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Simultaneous or sequential images with known defocus or other diversity.

Design constraints

Model accuracy, diversity choice, and scene content affect identifiability.
