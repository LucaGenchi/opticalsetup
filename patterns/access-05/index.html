Separate illumination and collection channels

The example

A central 405 nm delivery channel excites a fluorescent specimen; six surrounding collection fibers gather longer-wavelength return light and send it through a proximal rejection filter.

  1. Compare the distinct delivery and collection channels at the probe tip.
  2. Increase separation conceptually and discuss sampled-volume overlap rather than simply collection area.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 405 nm excitation (CW Laser) — Input is restricted to the central delivery channel.
  2. Central delivery fiber — Provides excitation at the distal end.
  3. Fluorescent tissue (Sample) — Signal is collected through a different physical channel.
  4. Six peripheral fibers — Summed collection sacrifices resolved per-fiber image information.
  5. Excitation rejection (Filter) — Blocks residual 405 nm light before detection.
  6. Proximal emission sensor (Photodetector) — Reads the collected emission band.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Central delivery fiber with surrounding collection fibers; double-clad architectures.

Key constraints

Collection area, background, channel isolation, and probe diameter trade against one another.
