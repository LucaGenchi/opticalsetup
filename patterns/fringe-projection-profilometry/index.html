Fringe projection profilometry

Recover surface geometry from deformation of projected patterns.

No setup available

The DMD does not project calibrated phase-stepped sinusoidal fringes onto a scattering height surface, and there is no phase-to-height acquisition/calibration model.

Arrangement and variants

Pattern projector, camera, calibrated geometry, and phase/shape reconstruction.

Design constraints

Phase ambiguity, motion, shadows, and nonuniform reflectance constrain recovery.
