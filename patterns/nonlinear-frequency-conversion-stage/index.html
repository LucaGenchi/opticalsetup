PULSE-09 · Pattern family · Setup available

Nonlinear frequency conversion stage

Coordinate interacting beams and a nonlinear medium to generate or amplify selected frequencies.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The crystal uses a user-set conversion share rather than a focused phase-matching calculation. It does not predict walk-off, depletion dynamics, damage thresholds or a measured conversion efficiency.

The setup

A 1064 nm pulse is focused into a native SHG crystal, recollimated and spectrally separated. The 532 nm channel reaches a bandpass and spectrometer; residual pump goes to a dump.

  1. Inspect the generated spectrum and the separate residual pump path.
  2. Set crystal conversion efficiency to zero and confirm that the harmonic channel disappears.

Components in this setup

  • Pulsed Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Crystal
  • Dichroic mirror
  • Filter
  • Spectrometer
  • Beam dump

Arrangement and variants

Polarization preparation, focusing, phase-matched medium, and output separation.
