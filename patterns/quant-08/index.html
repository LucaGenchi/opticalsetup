Counterpropagating optical lattice

The example

A 780 nm beam crosses an atom region and retroreflects through it; matched polarization and phase support an intended 390 nm lattice period.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 780 nm beam (CW Laser) — Incident lattice field.
  2. Polarization trim (λ/2 waveplate) — Align the counterpropagating field polarizations.
  3. Atom region — Potential exists only with coherent field overlap.
  4. Retro mirror (Mirror) — Return the beam through the same region.
  5. Mirror actuator — Translation changes standing-wave phase.
  6. Atom imaging (Camera) — Separate observation channel.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Counterpropagating or intersecting coherent beams with controlled frequency and polarization.

Key constraints

Relative phase, intensity, and detuning determine potential stability and geometry.
