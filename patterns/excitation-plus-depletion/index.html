Excitation plus depletion

Confine effective fluorescence by overlapping excitation with a shaped depletion field.

No setup available

There is no stimulated-depletion specimen response or vortex depletion field with a central vectorial intensity minimum.

Arrangement and variants

Aligned excitation and depletion beams with a designed depletion minimum.

Design constraints

Registration, timing, polarization, and dye response govern attainable contrast and resolution.
