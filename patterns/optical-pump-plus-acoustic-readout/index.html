SENSE-09 · Measurement architecture · No setup available

Optical pump plus acoustic readout

Generate an acoustic response by optical absorption and detect it separately.

No setup available

The registry contains no thermoelastic pressure source, acoustic propagation or ultrasound transducer. Optical sample emission cannot substitute for an acoustic readout.

Arrangement and variants

Pulsed optical delivery, acoustic receiver, and coordinated acquisition geometry.

Design constraints

Optical fluence, acoustic propagation, and detector response all influence the signal.

References

Applications
