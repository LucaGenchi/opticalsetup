The example

A monochromatic teaching soft-X-ray beam illuminates a zone plate with a central stop. An order-sorting aperture before the first-order focus rejects direct light and unwanted diffraction orders.

  1. Locate the central stop and order aperture as two different rejection operations.
  2. Increase bandwidth conceptually and discuss longitudinal chromatic smearing at the fixed sample plane.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Monochromatic input — Bandwidth must suit the strongly chromatic diffractive optic.
  2. Zone plate + central stop — The stop removes direct central transmission; zones create multiple orders.
  3. Desired first order — Converges toward the useful focus.
  4. Zero / higher orders — Undesired paths must be spatially excluded.
  5. Order-sorting aperture (Slit) — Passes the chosen cone close to the target.
  6. First-order sample focus (Sample) — Receives the selected diffractive order.
