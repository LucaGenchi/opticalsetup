SENSE-02 · Measurement architecture

Time-of-flight ranging

Infer path length from the measured delay of returned light.

The example

A 2 ns pulse illuminates a target 15 m away; the receiver compares the return with a launch timestamp, nominally 100 ns later.

  1. Derive the factor of two in the distance conversion.
  2. Compare pulse width with timing jitter rather than assuming 2 ns guarantees accuracy.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 2 ns source (Pulsed Laser) — Launch pulse with a proposed sharp timing edge.
  2. Transmit lens (Thin convex lens) — Deliver the outgoing pulse.
  3. 15 m target — Distance measured from the instrument reference plane.
  4. Receive lens (Thin convex lens) — Independent return aperture.
  5. Fast detector (Photodetector) — Timestamp returned pulse.
  6. Timing reference — Launch trigger defines t=0.
  7. Time-to-digital unit — Convert Δt to cΔt/2 after offset calibration.
