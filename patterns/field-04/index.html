FIELD-04 · Pattern family

Shearing interferometry

Interfere shifted or tilted copies of the same wavefront.

Schematic exampleLateral shear collimation test

The example

A weakly wedged plate produces two reflected copies of a 10 mm beam with a nominal 1 mm lateral shear, compared on an observation screen.

  1. Compare the two overlapping copies with a separate external reference wave.
  2. Keep overlap while adjusting a collimator; identify the shear direction before interpreting fringes.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 10 mm test beam (CW Laser) — Beam whose collimation is unknown.
  2. Wedged shear plate (Optical window) — Front and rear reflections overlap with lateral offset.
  3. First wavefront copy — Front-surface reflection.
  4. 1 mm shifted copy — Rear-surface reflection.
  5. Overlap screen (Camera) — Fringe direction changes with wavefront curvature.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Lateral, radial, or angular shear followed by recombination.

Key constraints

Shear sets sensitivity and spatial range; reconstruction requires boundary assumptions.
