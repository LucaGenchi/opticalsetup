WAVE-02 · Control architecture · Setup available

Multi-conjugate correction

Distribute correction across planes conjugate to different aberrating regions.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This is a physical two-corrector relay, not a claimed atmospheric tomography solution. No 9 km conjugation, multilayer wavefront reconstruction, automatic controller or corrected wide-field performance is calculated.

The setup

Two native reflective DMs use a two-lens relay with 100 mm focal lengths. The second DM sits 50 mm before the first-DM image plane, giving distinct corrective conjugates before a final camera.

  1. Identify the first DM plane and the second DM offset from the full 4f conjugate.
  2. Adjust the two native defocus controls independently and inspect final convergence; this does not supply a tomographic controller.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Deformable mirror
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Several guide measurements and correctors conjugated to different depths or altitudes.

Design constraints

Tomographic inference, conjugation accuracy, and guide coverage determine corrected field.
