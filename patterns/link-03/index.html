I/Q optical modulation

Control two field quadratures through coordinated modulation branches.

Schematic exampleNested I/Q transmitter at quadrature

The example

Two inner Mach–Zehnder channels encode I and Q; a π/2 phase offset precedes their final coherent combination.

  1. Inspect the separate I and Q drives.
  2. Reverse Q and identify the intended constellation reflection.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 1550 nm carrier (CW Laser) — Shared coherent carrier.
  2. Branch coupler (Beamsplitter) — Feed both nested modulators.
  3. I modulator (EOM) — Real-axis drive; example I=+1.
  4. I/Q combiner (Beamsplitter) — Add the two complex fields.
  5. Complex output — Example symbol +1+i, before normalization.
  6. Q modulator (EOM) — Imaginary-axis drive; example Q=+1.
  7. π/2 bias (Phase modulator) — Rotate the Q field by a quarter cycle.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Nested or parallel interferometric modulators with quadrature phase relation.

Key constraints

Branch balance, bias stability, and modulation bandwidth determine signal fidelity.
