CONTRAST-03 · Pattern family · Setup available

Dark-field collection

Exclude direct or specular light while collecting redistributed light.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The diffuser supplies a qualitative angular fan rather than particle-specific scattering. The setup demonstrates angular exclusion and collection, not a calibrated dark-field particle PSF.

The setup

A real diffuser scatters a narrow 532 nm beam; a central beam dump excludes the direct direction while an offset photodetector collects a portion of the angular fan.

  1. Inspect the traced rays reaching the offset detector and the centrally intercepted rays.
  2. Reduce diffuser divergence toward its minimum and compare off-axis signal; move the detector onto the direct axis to expose the rejected channel.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Diffuser
  • Beam dump
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Separate illumination and collection angles or place a stop over the direct beam.

Design constraints

Stray light and unwanted scattering can overwhelm the desired weak signal.
