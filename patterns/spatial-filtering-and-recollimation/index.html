BEAM-01 · Optical layout · Setup available

Spatial filtering and recollimation

Select a cleaner spatial distribution at a focus and recollimate it.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The slit represents a pinhole cross-section in 2D. Geometric clipping and recollimation are traced; Airy diffraction, coherent spatial-frequency cleaning and damage are not predicted.

The setup

A native 20 mm focusing lens, 0.5 mm meridional focal aperture and 100 mm recollimator form a physical spatial-filter bench at 532 nm.

  1. Inspect the tight geometric focus through the slit aperture.
  2. Shift the aperture away from the axis and compare transmitted power.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Slit
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Focusing optic, pinhole or slit, and collimator.

Design constraints

A smaller aperture rejects more structure but loses power and demands better alignment.
