ILL-05 · Pattern family · Setup available

Structured illumination / pattern projection

Project known spatial patterns for excitation, exposure, or measurement.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native binary micromirror routing and geometric projection are traced. No sinusoidal fringe synthesis, three-frame phase reconstruction, diffraction orders or structured-illumination resolution gain is calculated.

The setup

A real binary DMD selects a 10 mm pitch ON pattern and directs it downward through a 75 mm projection lens. The tilted receiving camera follows the tilted modulator-plane conjugacy.

  1. Inspect the surviving ON pattern and absorbed OFF regions.
  2. Translate the DMD by one third pitch along its active surface to compare the next phase position; keep the camera conjugate aligned.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • DMD
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Image a mask, DMD, or SLM onto the target; optionally step phase or orientation.

Design constraints

Projection contrast and calibration set performance; computational uses need a matching acquisition model.
