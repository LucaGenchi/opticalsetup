FIELD-07 · Optical layout · Setup available

Optical I/Q detection

Measure two quadratures to recover a complex optical signal.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native ray paths and camera coherent-field readout are computed within the existing scalar/coherence model. No RF electronics, noise, real coating phase, wavefront aberrations or calibrated interferometer sensitivity are inferred. This is a bench quadrature analyzer; complex-field fitting, matched balanced electronics and commercial integrated hybrids are not implemented.

The setup

A common coherent input feeds two real Mach–Zehnder analyzers. A 0.133 µm optical-path bias in the second analyzer introduces a nominal quarter-cycle phase at 532 nm and supplies four camera ports.

  1. Compare the two analyzer pairs and their distinct phase biases.
  2. Change a common test phase by editing both arm plates equally, retaining the quarter-wave offset.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser

  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Optical hybrid producing quadrature outputs with matched detector channels.

Design constraints

Quadrature error, branch imbalance, and phase recovery require calibration.
