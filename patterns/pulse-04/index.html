Nonlinear broadening plus compression

Follow the arrangement

  1. 300 fs input (Pulsed Laser) — Energy and beam size must keep the cell below damage and ionization limits.
  2. Multipass mirror A (Concave mirror) — Returns the beam through the nonlinear region.
  3. Gas broadening region (Gas cell) — Intensity-dependent phase generates additional bandwidth.
  4. Multipass mirror B (Concave mirror) — Several displaced passes distribute the interaction.
  5. Chirped-mirror compressor (Pulse compressor) — Adds dispersion opposite to the broadened pulse phase.
  6. Compressed output — Broad bandwidth permits but does not guarantee a short pulse.
  7. Spectrum + pulse check — Separate bandwidth evidence from duration evidence.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Nonlinear fiber, hollow-core guide, or multipass cell followed by dispersive compensation.

Key constraints

Ionization, self-focusing, mode quality, and phase structure bound useful broadening.
