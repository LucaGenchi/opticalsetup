The example

A high-NA objective traps a bead; a condenser relays forward scattered light to a quadrant position detector.

  1. Distinguish image position from pupil-plane position sensing.
  2. Describe the power-spectrum calibration required to infer trap stiffness.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 1064 nm trap (CW Laser) — Proposed trapping source.
  2. NA 1.2 objective (Objective) — Focus light into the bead plane.
  3. 1 µm bead (Sample) — Proposed dielectric particle in water.
  4. Collection condenser (Thin convex lens) — Collect transmitted and scattered fields.
  5. Quadrant detector (Photodetector) — Position-sensitive interference readout.
  6. Image camera (Camera) — Separate image branch checks trapping visually.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Tightly focused trapping beam with imaging or back-focal-plane detection.

Key constraints

Trap stability and force calibration depend on particle, medium, beam, and detector.
