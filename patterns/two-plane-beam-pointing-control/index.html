CONTROL-02 · Control architecture · Setup available

Two-plane beam-pointing control

Distinguish and correct beam displacement and angle using separate observations.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The actual mirrors, diagnostic split, lens and camera rays are represented. This bench is manually aligned; there is no calibrated actuator/sensor Jacobian, quadrant electronics or automatic two-axis pointing controller.

The setup

Two separated steering mirrors fold a beam through a monitoring split. One camera samples position in the outgoing beam; a lens and focal-plane camera provide the angle-sensitive branch.

  1. Rotate either steering mirror slightly and compare the two camera centroids; both actuators influence the outgoing position and angle.
  2. Move the far-field lens out of the sensing branch to demonstrate why that camera no longer isolates angular information.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Mirror
  • Beamsplitter
  • Camera
  • Thin convex lens

Arrangement and variants

Two suitably separated or conjugate position measurements with steering actuators.

Design constraints

Observability, cross-coupling, sensor noise, and actuator bandwidth govern correction.
