ILL-04 · Pattern family

Pupil-shaped illumination

Choose the angular illumination distribution by structuring a pupil plane.

Schematic exampleAnnular pupil illumination

Extended source — Supply a broad illumination pupil.

Open in full canvas ↗Download setup

Diagram scope: Normalized pupil radii specify a target distribution; no vector illumination field or annular diffraction is calculated. The editable diagram contains annotations and connection arrows; it does not run an optical simulation.

The example

An annulus occupying normalized pupil radii 0.6–0.8 illuminates a specimen with a selected cone of angles.

  1. Confirm that the annulus belongs to a pupil plane rather than a specimen image plane.
  2. Compare centering errors with changing inner and outer annular radii.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Extended source (Point source) — Supply a broad illumination pupil.
  2. 0.6–0.8 pupil annulus — Shape angular content at an aperture conjugate.
  3. Pupil relay (Thin convex lens) — Relays the annulus to the condenser pupil.
  4. Specimen (Sample) — Receives oblique illumination directions.
  5. Condenser pupil — Annulus must be centered in this conjugate.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Pupil masks, source shaping, or a modulator relayed to an illumination pupil.

Key constraints

Field uniformity, angular coverage, coherence, and throughput must be designed together.
