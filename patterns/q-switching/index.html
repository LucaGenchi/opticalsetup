CAV-06 · Pattern family · No setup available

Q-switching

Store energy in a gain medium before enabling rapid laser emission.

No setup available

The AOM can switch an optical path, but no gain medium stores inversion or releases a Q-switched laser pulse; a passive AOM cavity cannot implement Q-switching.

Arrangement and variants

Gain resonator with actively or passively controlled intracavity loss.

Design constraints

Gain dynamics and switching determine pulse formation; energy is initially stored in the medium.

References

  1. AA Opto-Electronic: acousto-optic Q-switch theory and loss control

Used in

Laser engineering · Materials processing and fabrication · Remote sensing and LiDAR

Applications

Energetic short pulses · laser processing · rangefinding

Related patterns

Stable optical resonator (CAV-01); Cavity dumping (CAV-07); Pulse picking and burst formation (PULSE-06).

Combine with

Stable optical resonator (CAV-01) for resonator geometry; Power stabilization loop (CONTROL-01) for output management.

What follows

Pulse conditioning and an application such as Time-of-flight ranging (SENSE-02) ranging or processing.
