FIELD-07 · Optical layout

Optical I/Q detection

Measure two quadratures to recover a complex optical signal.

The example

A coherent hybrid combines a signal and LO with phases 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°; two balanced pairs measure I and Q.

  1. Identify two detector pairs, representing four actual photodiodes.
  2. Compare I/Q gain imbalance and quadrature phase error before inferring a complex field.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Complex signal — Unknown signal phase relative to LO.
  2. Local oscillator (CW Laser) — Defines the quadrature reference.
  3. 90° optical hybrid — Four phase-related output ports.
  4. 0° / 180° pair (Photodetector) — Complementary real-quadrature outputs.
  5. 90° / 270° pair (Photodetector) — Complementary imaginary-quadrature outputs.
  6. I difference — Subtract 180° port from 0° port.
  7. Q difference — Subtract 270° port from 90° port.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Optical hybrid producing quadrature outputs with matched detector channels.

Key constraints

Quadrature error, branch imbalance, and phase recovery require calibration.
