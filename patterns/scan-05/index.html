The example

A pupil-matched objective pair images an axially translating remote mirror into the specimen space while the specimen objective stays fixed.

  1. Follow the round trip through the remote objective.
  2. Separate moving the remote mirror from physically moving the specimen objective.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Imaging beam — Beam enters the remote-focusing train.
  2. Remote objective (Objective) — Forms a focus near the translating mirror.
  3. Axial remote mirror (Mirror) — Translate ±20 µm around the conjugate focus.
  4. Pupil-matched relay (Thin convex lens) — Return travels to the specimen objective through matched pupils.
  5. Specimen objective (Objective) — Remains stationary.
  6. Shifted specimen focus (Sample) — Remote axial displacement maps into specimen space.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Matched remote objectives, moving remote reflector, or tunable focusing optics.

Key constraints

Pupil matching and refractive-index conditions control scan-induced aberrations.
