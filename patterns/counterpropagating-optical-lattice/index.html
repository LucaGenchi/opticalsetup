Counterpropagating optical lattice

Superpose coherent beams to create a periodic light-induced potential.

No setup available

The registry has no coherent standing-wave optical potential or atom/lattice interaction. Counterpropagating classical rays alone do not establish lattice sites or confinement.

Arrangement and variants

Counterpropagating or intersecting coherent beams with controlled frequency and polarization.

Design constraints

Relative phase, intensity, and detuning determine potential stability and geometry.
