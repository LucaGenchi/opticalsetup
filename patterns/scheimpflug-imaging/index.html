IMG-07 · Optical layout · Setup available

Scheimpflug imaging

Bring a tilted object plane into focus with coordinated optical and sensor tilts.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. This is a three-point plane-conjugacy example; it does not render a continuous tilted target or predict depth of field.

The setup

Three emitting points lie on a plane tilted 26.565° in the meridional section. A 100 mm lens images them onto a camera plane tilted by the opposite geometric slope.

  1. Follow the three narrow ray bundles to distinct positions on the tilted sensor.
  2. Set the camera rotation to zero and compare the off-axis bundle widths at the now incorrect plane.

Components in this setup

  • Object
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Tilted object, lens principal plane, and image plane with compatible conjugates.

Design constraints

The plane condition does not by itself guarantee acceptable depth of field or aberrations.
