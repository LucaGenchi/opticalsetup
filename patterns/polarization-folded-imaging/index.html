Polarization-folded imaging

Fold an imaging path through selective polarization transformations and reflections.

No setup available

The registry lacks a reflective-polarizer imaging cavity with a verified multiple-pass polarization-folded prescription. A lone PBS or folded mirror path would not be the specified pancake eyepiece.

Arrangement and variants

Retarders, polarization-selective reflectors, and powered surfaces in a folded path.

Design constraints

Throughput, polarization leakage, ghosts, and broadband retardance constrain quality.
