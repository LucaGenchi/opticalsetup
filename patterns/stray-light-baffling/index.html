Patterns / Correcting wavefronts and rejecting background

WAVE-08 · Pattern family · Setup available

Stray-light baffling

Intercept unwanted propagation paths before they reach sensitive optics or detectors.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native ray interception and clipping are computed. Coating ghosts, wall scatter, vane-edge diffraction and quantitative stray-light rejection are not predicted.

The setup

A real weak splitter branch terminates in a beam dump while two native slit plates bound the useful camera path. The accepted bundle remains traced through a relay lens.

  1. Identify the explicitly dumped optical branch and both aperture edges.
  2. Move a slit into the accepted bundle and compare detector throughput before calling the change better baffling.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Beam dump
  • Slit
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Field stops, vanes, beam dumps, blackened surfaces, and controlled apertures.

Design constraints

Baffles must reject unwanted paths without clipping the required field or adding scatter.
