SENSE-02 · Measurement architecture · Setup available

Time-of-flight ranging

Infer path length from the measured delay of returned light.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The native tracer carries optical-path pulse delay through a specular mirror return. It does not simulate diffuse lidar targets, a trigger discriminator, time-to-digital conversion, jitter or range estimation.

The setup

A 905 nm pulse is split into a direct reference and a mirror round trip. The reflecting target sits 300 mm from the send/return splitter; a second detector receives the returned pulse.

  1. Inspect the general detectors’ pulse records and compare the direct-reference and return path delays; account for their different detector distances.
  2. Move the target mirror 100 mm farther away. The returned pulse path grows by 200 mm, about 0.667 ns; rotating the target away removes the return.

Components in this setup

Arrangement and variants

Timed source, return collection, and synchronized detection.

Design constraints

Timing uncertainty, multipath, background, and repetition ambiguity affect range.
