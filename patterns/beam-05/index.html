The example

Two aspheric surfaces redistribute a nominal 3 mm radius Gaussian input into a 4 mm radius flat-top output without deliberately clipping the center.

  1. Compare redistribution with lossy aperture truncation.
  2. Change the assumed input radius and identify why a fixed remapper will no longer produce its design profile.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Gaussian input (CW Laser) — Design input intensity and radius are part of the prescription.
  2. Remapping surface (Aspheric lens) — Redirects annuli according to cumulative input power.
  3. Recollimating surface (Aspheric lens) — Restores a planar output wavefront.
  4. 4 mm top-hat — Target profile at the specified output plane.
  5. Cumulative power map — Equal power within corresponding input/output radii.
