Patterns / Recirculating and amplifying light

CAV-03 · Pattern family · Setup available

Power and signal recycling

Return selected interferometer outputs to alter stored power or signal response.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The mirrors implement real transmission/reflection paths. Resonant recycling gain, detuning, sideband response and precision-interferometer sensitivity are not computed.

The setup

The native Michelson receives a partially transmitting mirror at its input port and another at its readout port. Both form additional return paths around the central splitter.

  1. Inspect the extra optical round trips and the fraction escaping to the readout detector.
  2. Reduce one recycler reflectivity to zero and compare the corresponding returned path.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Mirror
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Recycling mirrors form coupled cavities around selected interferometer ports.

Design constraints

Coupled resonances and control interactions complicate bandwidth and noise optimization.
