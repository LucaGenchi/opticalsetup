BEAM-03 · Optical layout · Setup available

Mode matching

Match beam size, waist location, and wavefront curvature to a receiving mode.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. Native 2D geometrical rays are computed. Diffraction, target-image irradiance and real-lens aberrations are not calculated; ideal object-image markers are separate paraxial constructions. This is the adjustable injection-optics subsystem; no cavity eigenmode or Gaussian overlap efficiency is inferred from a ray waist.

The setup

A real two-lens 1064 nm injection train changes a collimated 6 mm beam into a converging bundle. The receiving camera inspects the chosen waist plane before a prospective cavity input.

  1. Read the profile near the receiving waist plane.
  2. Translate the second lens and compare both waist position and size at the fixed camera.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

One or more adjustable powered optics before a fiber, cavity, or gain region.

Design constraints

Alignment and phase-front overlap matter alongside geometric beam diameter.
