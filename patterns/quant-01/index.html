QUANT-01 · Optical layout

Heralded photon generation

Use detection in one output of a pair source to announce a correlated photon.

Schematic example405 nm heralded pair source

405 nm pump — Keep the proposed pair probability much below one per gate.

The example

A low-gain down-conversion source separates nominal 810 nm signal and idler photons; an idler click heralds the signal arm.

  1. Follow the herald separately from the delivered photon.
  2. Explain why narrowing a coincidence window cannot remove multipair emission.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 405 nm pump (CW Laser) — Keep the proposed pair probability much below one per gate.
  2. Pair crystal (Crystal) — Generate correlated signal and idler in a nonlinear crystal.
  3. Pump rejection (Filter) — Reject 405 nm before either counter.
  4. Signal output — Photon routed to the experiment.
  5. Signal counter (Photodetector) — Count signal events.
  6. Idler counter (Photodetector) — Herald detection arm.
  7. Coincidence gate — Example 2 ns coincidence window.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Pair-generation medium, pump rejection, separated outputs, and herald detector.

Key constraints

Multipair emission, losses, spectral correlations, and detector response affect heralded quality.
