SPECT-06 · Measurement architecture · Setup available

Fourier-transform spectroscopy

Recover spectral content from an interferogram measured versus optical delay.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This real bench demonstrates the interferogram acquisition geometry at one wavelength. It has no automated broadband interferogram sampling or Fourier-transform spectral reconstruction.

The setup

The native 633 nm Michelson combines fixed and variable optical paths at a photodetector. A delay-line element provides an editable optical-path offset in one arm.

  1. Change delay through fractions of a wavelength and compare the detector signal.
  2. Change source temporal coherence and separate fringe visibility from beam overlap.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Mirror
  • Camera
  • Mechanical delay line

Arrangement and variants

Variable-delay interferometer, detector, and Fourier reconstruction.

Design constraints

Delay range, sampling, phase correction, and source fluctuations affect spectral results.
