WAVE-06 · Optical layout · Setup available

Atmospheric dispersion compensation

Introduce angular dispersion opposing that accumulated in the atmosphere.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This is a physical 2D dispersive-prism pair. It does not model atmospheric refraction, counter-rotation about a 3D optical axis or automatically compensate a chosen zenith distance.

The setup

A visible native continuum passes two N-BK7 prisms in opposite orientations. Their refracted wavelength-dependent paths can be inspected at a downstream camera while prism position and angle are edited.

  1. Inspect the spectral fan after each prism and compare the camera spectrum.
  2. Rotate or displace the second prism and identify which colors miss it rather than assuming compensation from symmetry alone.

Components in this setup

  • Supercontinuum laser
  • Prism
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Counter-rotating dispersive prism assemblies or equivalent compensators.

Design constraints

Correction depends on observing geometry, wavelength range, and residual aberrations.
