Multispot illumination

Distribute excitation or processing light across several target points.

Schematic exampleFour-spot microlens illuminator

532 nm input — Illuminate all four lenslets with equal nominal irradiance.

The example

A 2 × 2 lenslet array creates four simultaneous focal spots; total incident power must be shared across the four channels.

  1. Count four channels even though the diagram groups them in two rows.
  2. Compare total laser power with per-spot power and the effect of unequal lenslet illumination.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 532 nm input (CW Laser) — Illuminate all four lenslets with equal nominal irradiance.
  2. 2 × 2 lenslets — Four physical subapertures partition the beam.
  3. Spot A / B — Upper row of two target spots.
  4. Spot C / D — Lower row of two target spots.
  5. Four-site specimen (Sample) — All four sites are illuminated simultaneously.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Diffractive fan-out, microlens arrays, or programmable holographic splitting.

Key constraints

Power per spot, uniformity, crosstalk, and unwanted orders limit scaling.
