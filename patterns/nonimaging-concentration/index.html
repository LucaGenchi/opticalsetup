Nonimaging concentration

Collect light over an angular range and guide it to a receiver.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This native 2D planar funnel is a nonimaging collection variant, not a compound-parabolic surface. Rays and reflection losses are computed; there is no 3D CPC concentration bound, diffraction, radiance calibration or etendue calculation.

The setup

Two 200 mm planar reflecting walls narrow a 40 mm entrance to a 10 mm exit. A 14 mm detector receives direct and wall-reflected rays from a collimated input.

  1. Compare the detector signal with the walls present and with both walls moved out of the beam.
  2. Change input direction or beam width to inspect finite angular and aperture acceptance rather than assuming perfect concentration.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Mirror
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Compound parabolic concentrators, Winston cones, and related edge-ray designs.

Design constraints

Acceptance angle, concentration, losses, and conservation of etendue set limits.
