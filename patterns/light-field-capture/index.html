Patterns / Encoding measurements for reconstruction

COMPUTE-06 · Measurement architecture · No setup available

Light-field capture

Sample ray position and direction to support view or focus reconstruction.

No setup available

No microlens array or angle-resolved 2D subimage detector exists. One thin lens and a one-dimensional camera profile cannot capture a light field.

Arrangement and variants

Microlens array near an image plane or equivalent angular sampling optics.

Design constraints

Finite sensor sampling trades spatial resolution against angular information.
