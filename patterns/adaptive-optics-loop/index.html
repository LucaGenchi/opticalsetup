Patterns / Correcting wavefronts and rejecting background

WAVE-01 · Control architecture · Setup available

Adaptive optics loop

Measure wavefront error and drive a corrector to compensate it.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The bench constructs common correction and a sensing branch. The sensor is a native focal camera, not a Shack–Hartmann reconstructor; feedback is manual and only tip/tilt and paraxial defocus are modeled.

The setup

A native deformable mirror applies reflective tip/tilt and defocus. A physical splitter sends corrected light to a science camera and an independent lens/camera sensing branch.

  1. Adjust DM tip/tilt and compare the image centroids in both physical branches.
  2. Use the sensing camera manually to restore alignment; then add a science-branch lens offset to expose a non-common-path error.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Deformable mirror
  • Beamsplitter
  • Camera
  • Thin convex lens

Arrangement and variants

Wavefront-sensing branch, controller, and pupil-conjugate deformable optic.

Design constraints

Latency, actuator range, sensing noise, and non-common-path errors limit correction.
