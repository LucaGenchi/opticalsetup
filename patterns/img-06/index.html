Reflective imaging relay

The example

A pair of concave mirrors carries a UV image through a folded relay, leaving a real intermediate focus available for a field stop.

Follow the arrangement

  1. UV object — A 266 nm emitting plane.
  2. Concave M1 (Concave mirror) — First curved reflector creates an intermediate image.
  3. Intermediate image — Place a field stop here to limit field extent.
  4. Concave M2 (Concave mirror) — Second reflector relays the image toward the detector.
  5. UV image (Camera) — Coated detector observes final conjugate.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Cassegrain, Gregorian, Ritchey-Chretien, and three-mirror anastigmat families.

Key constraints

Obscuration, field aberrations, alignment, and coating bandwidth remain design choices.
