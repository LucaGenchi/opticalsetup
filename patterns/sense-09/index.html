SENSE-09 · Measurement architecture

Optical pump plus acoustic readout

Generate an acoustic response by optical absorption and detect it separately.

Schematic exampleNanosecond optical pump with acoustic reception

5 ns pump — Short optical excitation pulse.

The example

A 532 nm, 5 ns pulse heats absorbing structures; a 5 MHz ultrasound receiver detects pressure transients while a photodiode monitors pulse energy.

  1. Separate the acoustic arrow from the optical pump path.
  2. Identify pulse-energy normalization before comparing absorber amplitudes.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 5 ns pump (Pulsed Laser) — Short optical excitation pulse.
  2. Energy pickoff (Beamsplitter) — Sample a small fraction before the tissue.
  3. Absorbing tissue (Sample) — Absorption launches a thermoelastic pressure wave.
  4. 5 MHz transducer — Receive acoustic rather than optical energy.
  5. Triggered acquisition — Record acoustic arrival waveform.
  6. Energy monitor (Photodetector) — Normalize pulse-to-pulse energy.
