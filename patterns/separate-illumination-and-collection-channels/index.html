Patterns / Transporting optics into constrained spaces

ACCESS-05 · Pattern family · Setup available

Separate illumination and collection channels

Allocate different probe channels to delivery and signal collection.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The setup uses scalar fiber coupling and qualitative isotropic specimen emission. It does not model a coherent image bundle, 3D collection solid angle or tissue-depth sensitivity.

The setup

One propagating fiber delivers 488 nm excitation to a fluorescent specimen. A physically separate fiber entry collects nearby emission and routes it through a long-pass filter to a detector.

  1. Inspect the two native fiber inputs, acceptance cones and output optics separately.
  2. Reduce collection input NA or move its entry away from the specimen and compare return signal.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Sample
  • Filter
  • Photodetector

Arrangement and variants

Central delivery fiber with surrounding collection fibers; double-clad architectures.

Design constraints

Collection area, background, channel isolation, and probe diameter trade against one another.
