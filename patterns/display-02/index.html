Follow the arrangement

  1. Image engine — Collimated input carrying angular image information.
  2. Input coupler (Diffraction grating) — Inject light into a guided path.
  3. Replica region 1 — Example extraction 1/3 of entering power.
  4. Replica region 2 — Extract half of the remaining power.
  5. Replica region 3 — Extract remaining nominal power.
  6. Pupil replica 1 — First accessible eye position.
  7. Pupil replica 2 — Second accessible eye position.
  8. Pupil replica 3 — Third accessible eye position.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Repeated partial extraction or diffractive pupil replication in one or two dimensions.

Key constraints

Expansion redistributes available light; uniformity, ghosting, and field coverage require design.
