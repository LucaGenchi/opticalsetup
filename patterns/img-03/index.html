Pupil relay

Image a pupil onto an accessible optical plane.

Diagram scope: Pupil placement is schematic; no high-NA objective aberration or pupil irradiance is computed. The editable diagram contains annotations and connection arrows; it does not run an optical simulation.

The example

A 75/150 mm relay maps a 4 mm scanner pupil onto an 8 mm objective pupil without treating that plane as a sample image.

  1. Compare the pupil conjugacy annotation with the intermediate field plane.
  2. Double the first pupil diameter and check whether the objective pupil would clip it.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Scanner pupil (Galvo mirror) — A 4 mm steering aperture; this is the relayed plane.
  2. 75 mm relay (Thin convex lens) — Scanner sits one focal length before this lens.
  3. Intermediate field — Shared focal plane; not the relayed pupil.
  4. 150 mm relay (Thin convex lens) — 225 mm from L1 gives 2× pupil magnification.
  5. Objective pupil (Objective) — 8 mm pupil, 150 mm after L2.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Relay optics between an entrance pupil and a scanner, stop, or corrector.

Key constraints

Pupil position and magnification must stay compatible with the field and apertures.
