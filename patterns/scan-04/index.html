The example

A nominal 100 mm f-theta lens maps ±5° optical scan angles to approximately ±8.73 mm of target displacement.

  1. Calculate 100 × 5π/180 mm and distinguish optical angle from mechanical galvo angle.
  2. Compare ideal fθ placement with measured field distortion and focus variation.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Expanded process beam (CW Laser) — Fill the specified scanning-lens pupil.
  2. ±5° optical scan (Galvo mirror) — Optical deflection is twice the mirror angle.
  3. 100 mm f-theta lens (Thin convex lens) — Design target is x = fθ, with θ in radians.
  4. ±8.73 mm target (Sample) — Flat processing field, nominally linear in angle.
  5. Angle command — Calibrate angle-to-position against the real lens.
