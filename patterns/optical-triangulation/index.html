SENSE-04 · Pattern family · Setup available

Optical triangulation

Recover position using separated observation or projection directions.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. This native example uses fluorescence as the observable isotropic spot rather than unmodeled elastic surface scattering. The 2D imaging geometry is useful for triangulation, but there is no calibrated pixel-to-height conversion, 3D baseline model or surface-dependent speckle.

The setup

A 488 nm laser excites a small fluorescent sample. A perpendicular 50 mm lens images the emission spot onto a camera; moving the sample along the laser axis shifts its camera position.

  1. Select the fluorescent specimen and move it 5 mm along the excitation axis; inspect the camera centroid shift.
  2. Set the fluorescence channel efficiency to zero to distinguish loss of observed emission from a geometrical height change.

Components in this setup

  • CW Laser
  • Sample
  • Thin convex lens
  • Camera

Arrangement and variants

Projector-camera or multi-camera geometry with calibrated baseline and imaging.

Design constraints

Occlusion, calibration, reflectance, and baseline trade range against precision.
