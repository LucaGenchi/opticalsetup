XRAY-07 · Pattern family · No setup available

Grating-based phase analysis

Encode phase gradients and small-angle scattering into measurable fringe changes.

No setup available

Source, phase and analyzer X-ray grating functions with Talbot interference and phase-stepped visibility readout are absent.

Arrangement and variants

Talbot or Talbot-Lau arrangements with phase/analyzer gratings and optional source grating.

Design constraints

Grating geometry, energy bandwidth, alignment, and stepping calibration limit sensitivity.

Combine with
