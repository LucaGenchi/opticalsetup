The example

A 0° polarizer followed by a 45° quarter-wave plate prepares circular input; a second quarter-wave plate and rotating analyzer measure its state.

  1. Distinguish preparation optics from the separate analysis optics.
  2. Compare readings with and without the analysis quarter-wave plate; linear analysis alone cannot determine circular handedness.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 532 nm source (CW Laser) — Input may have an imperfect polarization state.
  2. 0° preparation polarizer (Polarizer) — Defines horizontal linear polarization.
  3. 45° preparation QWP (λ/4 waveplate) — Prepares nominal circular polarization.
  4. Analysis QWP (λ/4 waveplate) — Convert elliptical components before linear analysis.
  5. Rotating analyzer (Polarizer) — Acquire several orientations.
  6. Power readings (Photodetector) — Intensity sequence supports calibrated Stokes estimation.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Waveplate sequences and analyzers; sequential or division-of-amplitude readout.

Key constraints

Calibration and the number of independent measurements determine recoverable information.
