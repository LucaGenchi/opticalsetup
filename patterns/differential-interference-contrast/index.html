CONTRAST-05 · Optical layout · No setup available

Differential interference contrast

Compare nearby optical paths through polarization splitting and recombination.

No setup available

Nomarski/Wollaston shearing prisms and polarization-dependent lateral shear are absent; ordinary prisms cannot replace them.

Arrangement and variants

Polarizers and matched shearing prisms around the specimen imaging system.

Design constraints

Contrast depends on shear direction and bias; birefringence can complicate interpretation.
