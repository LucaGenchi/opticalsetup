Patterns / Handling pulses and nonlinear interactions

PULSE-04 · Pattern family · Setup available

Nonlinear broadening plus compression

Generate additional bandwidth in a nonlinear medium and compensate the output phase.

Open setup in canvas ↗Download setup

Model limits. The hollow-core model is an approximate nonlinear broadening and chirp model. It does not establish ionization, damage margins, spatial beam quality or a physically measured compressed pulse.

The setup

Open the existing argon hollow-core fiber setup with physical coupling, a diagnostic split, negative-GDD compensation and autocorrelation readouts before and after compression.

  1. Inspect the native fiber gas, bore, coupling and computed loss before comparing its output spectrum.
  2. Set compressor GDD to zero and compare autocorrelation width; bandwidth alone does not establish compression.

Components in this setup

  • Pulsed Laser
  • Beamsplitter
  • Pulse compressor
  • Autocorrelator
  • Detector screen

Arrangement and variants

Nonlinear fiber, hollow-core guide, or multipass cell followed by dispersive compensation.

Design constraints

Ionization, self-focusing, mode quality, and phase structure bound useful broadening.
