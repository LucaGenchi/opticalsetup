PULSE-01 · Optical layout

Chirped-pulse amplification

Stretch an input pulse, amplify it, and recompress it afterward.

Schematic exampleStretch–amplify–compress 1030 nm chain

200 fs seed — Low-energy ultrashort input.

The example

An illustrative 200 fs seed is stretched toward 200 ps before amplification, then compressed; a diagnostic tap checks the final pulse rather than assuming the compressor restores the input.

  1. Separate pulse energy gain from pulse duration management.
  2. Check whether compressor phase cancels stretcher plus amplifier phase; equal nominal GDD alone may not cancel higher orders.

Follow the arrangement

  1. 200 fs seed (Pulsed Laser) — Low-energy ultrashort input.
  2. Positive-dispersion stretcher (Diffraction grating) — Target duration is 200 ps before the gain stage.
  3. Seeded amplifier — Adds energy while peak intensity is reduced.
  4. Negative-dispersion compressor (Pulse compressor) — Compensates the accumulated spectral phase.
  5. Experiment pulse — Delivered duration requires a measurement.
  6. Pulse diagnostic — Checks spectral phase and temporal pedestal independently.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Stretcher, amplifier chain, and matched compressor.

Key constraints

Residual spectral phase, gain narrowing, nonlinear phase, and damage limits constrain performance.
