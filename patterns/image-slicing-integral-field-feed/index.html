Patterns / Separating and measuring spectra

SPECT-03 · Pattern family · No setup available

Image slicing / integral-field feed

Rearrange a two-dimensional field into inputs suitable for spectroscopy.

No setup available

There is no image-slicer mirror assembly or spatially ordered pseudo-slit reformatting component.

Arrangement and variants

Image slicers, lenslet arrays, or fiber feeds forming spectrograph inputs.

Design constraints

Spatial sampling, slit packing, losses, and crosstalk compete for detector area.
