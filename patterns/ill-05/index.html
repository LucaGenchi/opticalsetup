The example

A DMD projects 10 µm-period stripes onto a sample with phases 0°, 120° and 240° for separate camera acquisitions.

  1. Follow the exposure synchronization as well as the optical path.
  2. Compare projected period at the sample with the pattern period on the modulator.

Follow the arrangement

  1. Green illumination (CW Laser) — Choose 532 nm for this concrete pattern projection.
  2. Three stripe patterns (DMD) — Sequential phases, not simultaneous frames.
  3. Projection relay (Thin convex lens) — Sets a 10 µm period at the specimen.
  4. Patterned specimen (Sample) — Pattern moves across the same field.
  5. Three camera frames (Camera) — Collect one frame per phase.
  6. 0° / 120° / 240° — Synchronize pattern changes and exposures.

Design choices

Arrangement and variants

Image a mask, DMD, or SLM onto the target; optionally step phase or orientation.

Key constraints

Projection contrast and calibration set performance; computational uses need a matching acquisition model.
