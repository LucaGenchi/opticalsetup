Patterns / Operating in X-ray and EUV regimes

XRAY-04 · Pattern family · No setup available

Distributed refractive power

Accumulate useful focusing power from a sequence of weak refractive elements.

No setup available

There are no X-ray wavelengths, refractive-decrement materials or selectable compound X-ray lens cartridges; visible glass lenses cannot substitute.

Arrangement and variants

Compound refractive lenses; transfocators insert or remove lens groups.

Design constraints

Absorption, chromaticity, apertures, and lens count limit usable focusing.

References

  1. Vaughan et al.: X-ray transfocators based on compound refractive lenses

Used in

X-ray and EUV instrumentation · Optical metrology

Applications

Hard-X-ray focusing · adjustable beamline focus

Related patterns

Orthogonal grazing-incidence focusing (XRAY-01); Mode matching (BEAM-03); Compensated zoom (IMG-09).

Combine with

Fixed-exit monochromation (SPECT-04) for energy selection; Mode matching (BEAM-03) for focus design.
