How the setup works

DMD holograms encode random-access positions and amplitudes at 22.7 kHz. Grating precompensation cancels the DMD carrier dispersion; it does not create temporal focusing at the sample.

  1. Chameleon Ultra II: 800 nm, 200 fs, 3.3 W
  2. Transmissive 1200 lines/mm grating and high-reflector M1
  3. L1=100 mm and L2=250 mm, 4f dispersion precompensation
  4. DLP4100 DMD: 1024×768, 13.68 µm pitch; binary holograms
  5. L3=200 mm, Fourier-plane filter selecting the first holographic order
  6. L4=200 mm to objective pupil
  7. Nikon 40×, NA 1.3 oil, WD 0.22 mm
  8. IP-Dip on XYZ stage

Observation and auxiliary paths

LED/50:50 beam splitter/dichroic and CCD provide epi observation through the same objective.

Limits and unresolved details

Native DMD ON/OFF micromirror routing is not binary holography. Carrier order 10 and selected holographic first order refer to different levels of the diffraction construction.

  • Repetition rate is not stated; instrument brand alone is not evidence for 80 MHz.
  • 3.3 W source output exceeds the lab handoff power range.
