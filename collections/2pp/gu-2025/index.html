Metalens arrays · 2025

3D nanolithography with metalens arrays and spatially adaptive illumination

10.1038/s41586-025-09842-x

Research retained · setup removed

The generated drawing has been removed. The source review below is preserved for a future individual reconstruction.

Current understanding

A spatially varying amplitude pattern illuminates independent metalenses. The metalenses make the foci; the SLM does not generate them as a holographic angular fan. Two benchmark points correspond to 50×50 NA 1 lenses and 370×350 NA 0.8 lenses.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Femtosecond laser: centre approximately 800 nm, 1 kHz, 7 W
  2. HWP1/PBS1 power control and beam expander
  3. PBS2/QWP1/reflective LC-SLM double pass: phase-to-amplitude modulation
  4. QWP2
  5. L1/L2 relay with vacuum cell around the intermediate focus
  6. Fold mirror
  7. Metalens array directly focuses into resist
  8. Substrate and translation stage above metalens array

Observation and auxiliary paths

A separate observation objective above the substrate connects to a beam splitter, LED, L3 and CMOS. It is not the writing objective.

Limits and unresolved details

No vectorial high-NA metalens field, spatially adaptive dose, meta-atom efficiency or vacuum propagation nonlinearities are solved. Only representative lenslets fit a 2D view.

  • Pulse duration is not verified in the downloaded supplement; do not invent 100 fs.

  • 7 W total output is outside the lab power range.
  • 50×50 array uses 200 µm lens size; 370×350 array uses 100 µm lens size.
