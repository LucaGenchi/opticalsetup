How the setup works

Binary holographic foci can write with one amplified pulse. The authors analyze high-order nonlinear response, rather than assuming a calibrated pure two-photon reaction.

  1. Spitfire Pro: 800 nm, 100 fs, 1 kHz, 4 W
  2. HWP/PBS attenuation
  3. Reflective 600 lines/mm grating
  4. L1=225 mm and L2=250 mm dispersion compensation relay
  5. DLP6500 DMD: 1920×1080, 7.56 µm pitch, laser synchronized
  6. L3=150 mm, Fourier-plane order filter
  7. L4=200 mm and dichroic
  8. Nikon 40× NA 1.3 oil objective, WD 0.24 mm
  9. Resin on six-axis hexapod

Observation and auxiliary paths

589 nm fibre LED below the substrate reflects from M1 through the objective and dichroic to L6=100 mm and CCD.

Limits and unresolved details

Single-pulse initiation, hologram optimization, high-order response and multi-focus dose are outside the workbench tracer and destination single-focus educational model.

  • Supplement compensation prose swaps the L1/L2 values; the drawing follows the explicit Fig. S1 caption (225/250 mm).
  • 4 W is source output; reported 5 nJ per focus at 1 kHz is not source power.
