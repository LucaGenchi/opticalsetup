Current understanding

Stage-synchronized image masks and continuous Z translation remove interlayer dead time. The dispersive DMD and objective establish a temporal focus at the projected image plane.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Spitfire amplifier: 800 nm, 65 fs, 5 kHz, approximately 22 nm bandwidth
  2. HWP and PBS power control
  3. Galilean expansion: −75 mm / +100 mm
  4. πShaper 6_6_TiS flat-top shaping
  5. L1=100 mm and L2=150 mm expansion
  6. DLP3000 DMD, approximately 24° incidence; up to 4 kHz masks
  7. L3=300 mm collecting diffracted light along the DMD normal
  8. 100×, NA 1.49 Nikon dip-in objective
  9. BBK/PETA resin on 3-axis air-bearing stage

Observation and auxiliary paths

50:50 beam splitter before the objective feeds a CCD. A 633 nm alignment laser enters through a DMLP650 dichroic. A GaP detector is used upstream of the DMD while minimizing pulse duration.

Limits and unresolved details

No time-dependent field or curing model is solved. The drawing identifies the spatial conjugate planes without replacing the DMD with a generic periodic amplitude mask simulation.

  • 5 kHz is outside the lab repetition-rate range.
  • Total operating source power is not a single value.
