Depletion-assisted writing · 2011

Three-dimensional direct laser writing inspired by stimulated-emission-depletion microscopy

10.1364/OME.1.000614

Research retained · setup removed

The generated drawing has been removed. The source review below is preserved for a future individual reconstruction.

Current understanding

A central phase step in the depletion pupil produces axial and radial inhibition around the excitation volume. This is not simply a lateral doughnut.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Mai Tai HP excitation, approximately 810 nm
  2. Excitation AOM: 4 kHz, 3% duty
  3. 532 nm continuous-wave depletion laser and synchronized AOM
  4. Central 180° phase mask imaged onto the objective entrance pupil
  5. Combined, circularly polarized beams
  6. Leica HCX PL APO, NA 1.4
  7. DETC/PETA resin, scanned at 100 µm/s

Evidence inspected

  • Author manuscript pp. 6–7: apparatus description; p. 7 Fig. 2: calculated excitation/depletion focal distributions. Fig. 2 is not an optical-train drawing.

Limits and unresolved details

No stimulated depletion, vectorial PSF, triplet dynamics or resin kinetics are simulated. Combiner placement is inferred from the stated common objective, not a published mechanical drawing.

  • Excitation repetition rate and pulse duration are not specified in the retrieved manuscript.
  • Depletion 50 mW is before the objective; ordinary excitation 7.4–8.3 mW is not a single fixed source power.
  • Central mask occupies 50% of the pupil area; the 2D drawing cannot encode radial area fractions.
