Try three controls

  1. Source emission and power. Switch off the pulse train or set power to zero to remove arrivals. Positive source power is metadata; relative attenuation is controlled by the polarization optics.
  2. Galvo center angle. Change either mirror angle and inspect the shifted sample arrival while the beam stays centered in the pupil.
  3. Stage depth and relay spacing. Play the Z motion to see defocus spread, or shift the pupil relay to produce pupil walk.

Current understanding

A focused pulse supplies a localized two-photon excitation region. Pupil-conjugate mirrors move its geometric focus while the stage changes sample depth. The two scanner axes are projected onto this single optical section.

Optical sequence

  1. Femtosecond source
  2. Half-wave plate and linear polarizer
  3. 2× beam expander
  4. X galvo, 1:1 inter-pivot relay, Y galvo
  5. 40:80 mm scan/tube pupil relay
  6. NA 0.65 dry objective
  7. Photoresist on an axially moving stage

Design basis

  • Paper-independent paraxial design. Both scanner pivots are conjugate to the objective back focal plane. All operating values and lens choices are illustrative.
