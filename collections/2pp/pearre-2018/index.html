Current understanding

A fast resonant raster is intensity-addressed by the Pockels cell. The 3.33 MHz command stream is not the optical repetition frequency.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Ti:sapphire: typically 780 nm, approximately 120 fs, 80 MHz
  2. Pockels-cell intensity control (ConOptics 350-80/302RM)
  3. Power-monitor pickoff
  4. 2× Galilean beam expander
  5. 7.91 kHz resonant X scanner and slow Y galvo
  6. Scan/tube relay
  7. 25×, NA 0.8 objective with immersion compensation ring
  8. Resin; objective piezo Z and positioning stage

Observation and auxiliary paths

Dichroic/PMT imaging shares the objective path; PMT is an imaging return detector, not a writing source.

Limits and unresolved details

Pockels intensity control is represented by the documented assembly, not a standalone phase-only modulator. Resonance, scan linearization and PrintImage software are not reproduced.

  • Laser output is approximately 0.6–1 W, not a single fixed setting.
  • 6–10 W is pump power, not the printing beam.
