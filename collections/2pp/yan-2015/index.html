Current understanding

An SLM phase pattern creates a 4×4 hexagonal multifocal array, with the zeroth order filtered at a Fourier plane before the final objective focal plane. Parallel fabrication addresses the serial-writing bottleneck and can produce simple and compound parabolic-mirror arrays. The 633 nm value is optical characterization, not evidence for the fabrication laser wavelength.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Fabrication laser source (wavelength, timing and power unspecified)
  2. SLM with phase modulation pattern for a 4×4 hexagonal multifocal array
  3. Zeroth-order filtration at a Fourier plane
  4. Objective lens (specification unavailable)
  5. Parallel focal array at the objective focal plane
