SLM holography · 2024

High-Throughput Two-Photon 3D Printing Enabled by Holographic Multi-Foci High-Speed Scanning

10.1021/acs.nanolett.4c00505

Research retained · setup removed

The generated drawing has been removed. The source review below is preserved for a future individual reconstruction.

Current understanding

Interference-aware CGHs suppress the SLM zero-order contribution near the pattern centre. The AOM zero order is the useful writing path, a separate meaning of zero order.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Menlo BlueCut: 1030 nm, 400 fs, 1 MHz; maximum 10 W
  2. AOM shutter: zeroth order writing, first order dumped
  3. HWP/PBS attenuation
  4. Beam expander and iris
  5. Reflective Hamamatsu X15213-03BL SLM; 1272×1024, 12.5 µm pitch, 60 Hz
  6. 300/200 mm 4f relay
  7. Orthogonal galvo pair
  8. 150/150 mm 4f relay to objective pupil
  9. Dichroic and Olympus 60× NA 1.35 oil objective
  10. SZ2080; piezo Z with manual XY

Observation and auxiliary paths

Dichroic, L5 and CCD monitor the sample through the writing objective.

Limits and unresolved details

Do not add a spatial stop at the hologram centre: cancellation is computational interference. The existing tracer cannot optimize that coherent hologram.

  • 10 W is maximum capacity, not measured operating power.
  • 1 MHz is below the lab handoff range.
  • Pupil illumination is 8.5 mm against an 8.1 mm entrance aperture.
