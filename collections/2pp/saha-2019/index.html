Current understanding

A 4f-like arrangement images the DMD mask to the build plane. Spectral path lengths are equal at that plane, where the pulse is shortest; adjacent planes have lower peak intensity.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Amplified femtosecond source: 800 nm, nominal 35 fs, 1 kHz
  2. DMD image mask and angular dispersion
  3. Collimating lens L1
  4. Fold mirror
  5. 60×, NA 1.25 objective L2
  6. Projected resin plane on XYZ stage

Limits and unresolved details

Geometric rays cannot establish temporal focusing, sheet confinement, voxel size or polymer conversion. No generic pulse compressor is substituted for this effect.

  • 35/39/42 nW per projected pixel are measured before the objective; they are not total source power.
  • Supplementary instrument prescription has not been downloaded.
  • 1 kHz and 35 fs fall outside the current lab input ranges.
