Commercial galvo writing · 2014

An elasto-mechanical unfeelability cloak made of pentamode metamaterials

10.1038/ncomms5130

Research retained · setup removed

The generated drawing has been removed. The source review below is preserved for a future individual reconstruction.

Current understanding

Dip-in writing and long working distance allow millimetre-scale metamaterial construction; reported writing speed is 5 cm/s, with 0.5 µm lateral and 1 µm axial line spacing.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Frequency-doubled erbium-fibre laser: 780 nm, approximately 90 fs
  2. Nanoscribe Photonic Professional GT internal beam delivery (undisclosed)
  3. Galvo scanning with positioning stages
  4. Zeiss 25×, NA 0.8 dip-in objective
  5. IP-S resin on glass
