Acousto-optic scanning spatial-switching multiphoton lithography

AOD scanning with spatial switching · 2023 · 10.1088/2631-7990/ace0a7

Research notes · native setup pending

The source review below is preserved for an individual reconstruction.

Current understanding

The DOE makes eight foci; the DMD independently gates their scanned image regions. The fast AOD scans a line in 17.5 µs; nonlinear RF sweeps compensate changing cylindrical power.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Keyun laser: 517 nm, 250 fs, 45 MHz, 6.5 W
  2. N-F2 prism pair P1/P2 for group-delay precompensation
  3. AOM temporal gate, approximately 2 MHz switching
  4. N-F2 prism P3 for central-field angular precompensation
  5. AOD X step addressing and AOD Y nonlinear frequency sweep
  6. Eight-beam DOE adjacent to AOD Y exit pupil
  7. Custom Kepler dispersion compensation module
  8. CL1=250 mm at front focal plane of TL1
  9. DLP6500 DMD spatial mask at back focal plane of TL1
  10. TL2 and Olympus 100× NA 1.4 objective
  11. IP-L/IP-Dip on moving XYZ stage

Limits and unresolved details

The current AOD is an ideal angle controller, not a nonlinear acoustic-wavefront solver. The drawing keeps the specialized compensation and mask planes explicit.

  • KDCM prescriptions need the supplement.
  • 6.5 W is source output; 260 mW for eight foci is a different measurement plane.
  • Reported 532 nm laser is for wavefront metrology, not the 517 nm fabrication path.
