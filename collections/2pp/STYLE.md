# 2PP collection style

This collection is one educational introduction plus five distinct research
architectures. Its native scenes use the same visual and interaction rules.

## Composition

- Use one landscape Figure frame, approximately 4:3 to 3:2. Keep the optical
  bench compact with genuine folding mirrors. Preserve optical path lengths
  and conjugate planes when folding; label added folds as interpretation.
- Put the pulsed source at the upper left. Reading order is left to right,
  then down a folded bench, ending at the objective and resin in the lower
  right. A different published topology may require another fold.
- Place a short title at the top and one short explanation block beside or
  below the bench. Keep long evidence tables and control instructions on the
  companion page. A scene should fit without horizontal scrolling.
- Use native components, native wavelength-colored rays and native Figure
  frames. Never draw beam-path annotations that could be mistaken for rays.
- Keep labels concise and use one label size per scene, sized to be legible
  when fitted. Title: 1.6 times body size. Interpretation notes: body size,
  muted slate. Match perceived text sizes across frames, even when reported
  focal lengths require different physical footprints.
- Show the writing objective, its actual geometric focus, and a mounted resin
  sample consistently. The resin marker means a qualitative pulsed arrival.
  It is not a calculated voxel. Observation branches use short secondary
  labels and their real traced wavelengths; do not fake a return signal.

## Provenance and limits

Use `Reported` for a value verified in the paper and `Design choice` for an
inferred spacing, prescription, fold or operating point. Every paper scene
includes the key: `Design choice = free interpretation; not specified in the
paper.` An approximate value or a bound is never presented as an exact input.
The Basic scene uses illustrative values throughout and carries no paper claim.

Every scene includes a concise `Geometric model` note for its central limit.
The collection never implies it computes arbitrary 3D CGH fields, vectorial
high-NA PSFs, calibrated voxel dimensions, polymerization kinetics, depletion
chemistry, temporal-focusing confinement, metalens electromagnetic fields,
proximity effects or calibrated throughput. Phase and spectral displays show
configured masks or sampled rays, not a reconstructed wave field.

## Interaction

Each scene offers three documented experiments: source power/on-off, its
distinguishing mechanism, and a geometry/scan/pattern control. The initial
state should immediately show that distinguishing mechanism.

Animate only a physical mechanism: mirror rotation, stage translation, or
discrete programmed frames. A mask display must show the current 2D frame;
the internal 1D tracer sample is a stated section through that frame. Do not
use continuous decorative stripe translation. Separate X and Y controls must
state how an out-of-plane axis is represented in this 2D workbench.

## Acceptance

Run the native tracer with the default state and all three controls. Verify
sample arrivals, finite geometry, relay conjugacy where relevant, and save /
reload. Inspect the real native editor at desktop width and a 1024 px layout,
including palette, toolbar, inspector, labels and console. Use screenshots
from that browser; a rendered SVG alone is not browser evidence.
