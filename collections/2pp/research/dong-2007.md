# Dong 2007 — evidence and usage note

## Status

This is a **mechanism interpretation, not an exact apparatus reconstruction**. The complete article, supplement, and apparatus figure were not available after targeted exact-title/DOI searches of the publisher, author listing, and public repository results on 2026-09-05.

Paper identity: X.-Z. Dong, Z.-S. Zhao, and X.-M. Duan, “Micronanofabrication of assembled three-dimensional microstructures by designable multiple beams multiphoton processing,” *Applied Physics Letters* **91**, 124103 (2007), DOI [10.1063/1.2789661](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2789661).

The benchmark DOI `10.1063/1.2535504` identifies a different paper and is not used.

## Implemented source-to-sample path

1. An enabled native pulsed laser emits a finite-width sampled beam.
2. A native afocal lens pair expands the beam by approximately 3×.
3. A real fold mirror packs the layout and redirects the computed rays.
4. A native slit acts as the adjustable 1D section of an aperture mask.
5. A native microlens array assigns the transmitted rays to four independent lenslet axes.
6. Each illuminated group converges at the native photocurable-resin stage. The default 60 mm lenslet focal length places the paraxial focus at the resin plane.
7. The stage moves transversely as an explicitly invented writing-motion demonstration.

The fold, prescription, source values, mask opening, focus count, and stage motion are all labelled on the canvas as free interpretation where applicable.

## Controls verified

| Control | Change | Expected and supported visible result |
|---|---|---|
| Laser enable | `enabled: true → false` | All traced rays, pulse tracks, and resin write markers disappear. |
| Aperture selection | slit gap `38 → 18 mm` | Outer array illumination is blocked; four lenslet focus markers reduce to the two central foci. |
| Lens-set geometry | lenslet count `4 → 2` | The illuminated section becomes two independently focused beamlets. This is a 1D section, not a 2D focus grid. |
| Focus-plane mismatch | focal length `60 → 45 mm` | Beamlet waists move 15 mm before the resin, and the rays arrive diverging instead of meeting at the sample plane. |

## What the model does not prove

- No 2D or 3D array field is calculated; the workbench shows one meridional section.
- No diffraction, vectorial PSF, aberration, interference, focus uniformity, or aperture-edge diffraction is calculated.
- No multiphoton absorption, dose accumulation, threshold curing, voxel dimension, resin chemistry, or mechanical assembly is calculated.
- The preview power is qualitative and is not sample power or calibrated per-focus power.
- No observation or alignment return path is included because none is established by the accessible abstract.

## Companion calculator

The scene intentionally does not claim a literature-backed numerical handoff. All source values are invented for demonstration, the 1 MHz repetition rate lies outside the companion lab’s accepted 10–100 MHz range, no objective NA is verified, and total source power cannot be converted into per-focus sample power from the available evidence.
