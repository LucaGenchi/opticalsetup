**Primary evidence:** Somers et al., [“Rapid, continuous projection multi-photon 3D printing enabled by spatiotemporal focusing of femtosecond pulses”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-021-00645-z), *Light: Science & Applications* 10, 199 (2021). Inspected the attached 11-page publisher PDF, including a rendered apparatus page: p. 2 Fig. 1a–b and caption; p. 3 Results and numerical-model description; p. 9 “3D printing system.” SHA-256: `9fff3a13ae10d356f028575af9151c9c46094c3eb73da4ac31732d2f5417e7ba`. Also read the branch evidence note, scene, DMD/tracer/stage implementation and tests, plus the integration style, review protocol and image-plane criterion.

The paper supports Spitfire 800 nm, 65 fs, 5 kHz and approximately 22 nm bandwidth; HWP/PBS power control; −75/+100 mm expansion to approximately 6 mm; πShaper; +100/+150 mm expansion; DLP3000 at approximately 24° incidence with the selected blaze order normal to its face; 300 mm collecting achromat; Nikon 100×/NA 1.49 dip-in objective; BBK/PETA on a three-axis air-bearing stage. Methods also identify the BSW29/CCD observation path, 633 nm/DMLP650 alignment branch, and insertable GaP diagnostic. The native train preserves that order. The πShaper annotation and disconnected diagnostic are appropriately explicit about untraced behavior. The CCD carries incident illumination only, with a clear warning that it does not reproduce the paper's sample-return image.

Source power, input diameter, relay placements, objective EFL/WD, aperture sizing, carrier density/order and playback settings remain inferred. In particular, the 30 mm DMD face and 6 mm stripe pitch are display/section choices, not reported DLP3000 dimensions. The source’s approximately 22 nm bandwidth should retain its approximation. One correction: p. 9 explicitly **assumes 70% objective transmission** for estimating print-plane intensities; the scene uses exactly 70% but incorrectly includes transmission among values unspecified by the paper. Label it a reported assumption, not a measured calibration.

**Image-plane audit:** this is DMD field-plane-to-resin imaging. It is not a scanner-to-objective-pupil relay. The DMD active face is local x=−9, world y=735; L3 is y=435; the objective equivalent plane is y=132.87, and the nominal resin plane is y=130.87. Thus the actual propagation distances are 300 / **302.13** / 2 mm, not the 302 mm middle distance asserted in the branch note. With focal lengths 300 and 2 mm, the complete matrix is approximately `[−0.006666667, 0; 0.000216667, −150]`. B=0 gives correct image conjugacy; C need not vanish for this field-image requirement. Five independent native incident line probes at DMD field offsets −2, −1, 0, +1 and +2 mm produced magnification −1/150, with all three admitted spectral samples coincident at each image point. The 24° incidence and normal central exit pass the branch’s direct traced-angle test. Spectral separation at the pupil is intentional.

**Mechanism honesty:** the original DMD has a continuously advancing 1D modulo phase and translated bright/dark intervals. It has no discrete 2D programmed frames. Calling that a mask “sequence” invites a conveyor interpretation and does not reproduce Fig. 1b. The 0.4 Hz masks and 0.2 Hz triangular Z traversal share a display clock but do not implement the externally triggered pattern/motion synchronization described on p. 9. The stage reverses every 2.5 s; it is not continuous one-way fabrication. The browser explicitly says “mechanics illustrative · pulses not synced.” At all tested depths the native pulse remains configured as 65 fs with no computed stretched duration, while representative arrival weight stays unchanged. Wavelength overlap, moving resin marks and schematic pulse packets cannot establish temporal recompression, axial confinement, layer thickness, curing or throughput. The existing canvas and inspector limitations acknowledge most of this, but are too small at overview to carry that burden effectively.

Somers is a strong candidate for the collection's projection-family slot because its Methods provide more apparatus detail than the current Saha evidence note and because continuous triggered projection adds a useful extension. Somers p. 2 explicitly relates this work to the 1 kHz, interrupted layer-by-layer predecessors; p. 10 identifies Saha as reference 43. Retaining both solely for the same DMD spectral fan adds little. Compare the finished Saha review before making the five-paper selection. Shared issues are device-frame semantics, reference-arrival versus image claims, legible limitation text, objective aperture behavior and pulse/stage playback semantics; they belong in the shared implementation, not a Somers-specific component fork.

Replace the stripe-conveyor implication and expose the limits of the reversing stage and unsynchronized pulse preview next to the mechanism. Recompose with readable labels and a clearly visible mask, correct transmission/aperture provenance, finish the outstanding original browser checks, and repeat meaningful native/browser checks after integration.
