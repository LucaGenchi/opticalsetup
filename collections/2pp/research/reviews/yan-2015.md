## Source and mechanism

The [IOP publisher preview on ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279969832_High-throughput_fabrication_of_micrometer-sized_compound_parabolic_mirror_arrays_by_using_parallel_laser_direct-write_processing)
was independently readable: first page and Figures 1–4 captions. It verifies
Yan, Cumming and Gu, *Journal of Optics* 17, 075803,
[DOI 10.1088/2040-8978/17/7/075803](https://doi.org/10.1088/2040-8978/17/7/075803).
Figure 1 supports an SLM phase pattern, a 4×4 hexagonal multifocal array,
Fourier-plane zeroth-order filtering and the objective focal plane. This is
SLM holographic parallelization; neither a separate fabricated DOE nor a
partitioned lenslet array is established by the accessible evidence.

The preview explicitly assigns **633 nm to optical characterization of the
fabricated mirrors**, not fabrication. Its 17.8 µm FWHM at 150 µm height and
68.3% transmission are mirror-performance measurements, not writing-voxel
size or fabrication efficiency. No complete article, methods, apparatus image
or supplement was obtained. IOP article/supplement and RMIT requests failed;
ResearchGate figure-image/full-text download links failed. The older note's
empty-repository-files finding was not independently reverified.

The branch correctly labels itself a mechanism interpretation and disables
both paper presets and configured-value handoff. Source wavelength 800 nm,
150 fs, 80 MHz, 0.1 W, 6 mm beam, transmissive SLM, orders `-3,-1,1,3`,
70 lines/mm, 10% zero order, two 60 mm lenses, all spacing, fold, objective
EFL 40 mm/WD 10 mm/air NA 0.8, generic resin and stage raster are **design
choices**, not recovered operating values. The grating layer branches each
incident ray into angular orders; it does not calculate the reported phase
pattern, a 2D hexagonal field or parabolic-mirror fabrication.

**The stop unintentionally clips desired inner orders.** Its native absorber
occupies x=370…390, y=297…303; it is a 20 mm deep block, not a thin Fourier
stop. Inner bundles focus at x=380, y=300±3.365281, but several rays meet the
block before or after that plane. At the two beam edges neither inner order
survives. The original tests establish convergence of surviving rays, not
full-width transmission; four reference markers conceal the loss.

The SLM's actual active plane is x=251, not its centre x=260. L1/L2 are
120 mm apart and preserve collimation; the objective equivalent plane y=204
does focus surviving orders on the nominal resin y=164. There is no scanner.
The SLM-to-objective-BFP matrix is `[-1,-85;0,-1]`, so this is not a pupil
conjugate relay. The native stop is clamped to y=210 rather than the nominal
BFP y=244. Full-width outer bundles extend to ±17.485888 mm at the BFP and
±23.280243 mm at the seated stop, inside its 32 mm radius; downstream pupil
clipping does not explain the lost inner rays.

Native animation functions were also sampled at 0, 2.5, 5 and 10 s. The
configured raster puts resin y at 162, 163, 164 and 166 mm respectively,
with four reference markers throughout. At ±2 mm defocus, actual spans are
0.30 mm outer / 0.1875 mm inner. Thus the constant label “4 focal-plane hits”
overstates most animation frames. XY is one in-plane lateral axis; Z moves
the physical sample plane. Neither is a recovered writing trajectory.
