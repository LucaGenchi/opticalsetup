The 35 fs value is verified in this primary source; the older foundation omission is stale.

Methods p. 19 gives ML2's **75:200 mm** relay and **3×3 pixels per 100 µm, NA 0.8 lens**, versus ML1's 200:200 relay and 16×16 pixels per 200 µm, NA 1.0 lens. The objective/BS/LED/L3/CMOS branch observes printing; **it is not the writing objective**. The [official 28-page supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-025-09842-x/MediaObjects/41586_2025_9842_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), hash `638eaae37d600d6e77b379218af9f61ed914d5874e7df9cec468267d3b67d979`, verifies the 20 nm spectral FWHM and measured 49.3% focusing efficiency on p. 4, and array dimensions/short working-distance constraints on pp. 12–13.

The original models the sequence with native components and an offset observation sample. Its SLM `amplitude` levels multiply **intensity**, not field amplitude; no angular fan or CGH solution is created. Double-pass polarization routing is present, but pixel Jones retardance, calibrated grayscale conversion and handedness-dependent metalens efficiency are not solved.
