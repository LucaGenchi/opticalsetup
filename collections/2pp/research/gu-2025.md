# Gu 2025 — addressable metalens foci

Songyun Gu et al., “3D nanolithography with metalens arrays and spatially adaptive illumination,” *Nature* 648, 591–599 (2025), [DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09842-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09842-x).

The integrated scene demonstrates **an intensity image selecting independent metalens foci**. The SLM does not synthesize a multifocus CGH: the metalenses supply the independent focusing axes. This is an editable geometric section, not a calibrated printing system.

**Design choice = free interpretation; not specified in the paper.** This key covers window glass/thickness and its compensation, unreported free-space spacings, the seven-lens representative section, enlarged lenslet dimensions, the illustrative pixel frames, stage motion, optical loss settings and sample termination.

The complete topology is source → HWP1/PBS1 → expander → PBS2/QWP1/SLM → QWP1/PBS2 output → QWP2 → L1/vacuum cell/L2 → fold mirror → metalens array → resin/substrate. The observation microscope is beyond the substrate and uses objective, BS, LED, L3 and CMOS. **Its objective does not focus the writing beam.** No sample return or camera signal is synthesized. ML1 remains documented by the paper, but its unused replica was removed from this scene.

## Field conjugacy and dimensional limits

The reflective SLM surface is at **x = 565 mm**, 9 mm left of its element center. The folded path to L1 is 30 + 45 = **75 mm**. L1/L2 focal lengths remain the reported **75/200 mm**; their physical separation is **275.623837986 mm**. The two design-choice 1 mm fused-silica windows have `n(800 nm) = 1.453317255`, so adding `2 × (1 − 1/n) = 0.623837986 mm` compensates their reduced paraxial propagation distance. L2→fold→array remains **60 + 140 = 200 mm**. The output fold puts the writing region at lower right without changing those path lengths.

Native small-angle probes in the SLM and array local coordinates give `A = −200/75`, `B ≈ 0`, `C ≈ 0`, `D = −75/200`. This is **SLM field→array imaging**, not scanner→objective-pupil conjugacy. Finite-angle window refraction leaves a maximum 800 nm sample support of **0.0001591 mm** and maximum departure from an ideal lenslet axis of **0.000276 mm** in the drawing; it is not an exactly corrected optical prescription. The 20 nm spectrum gives up to **0.2761 mm** geometric support at the nominal plane.

The representative array uses **9 mm pitch and 40 mm focal length**, independently enlarged from the reported **100 µm, NA 0.8** devices. Its drawn air-side marginal angle corresponds to about NA 0.112; the reported NA is provenance, not a property calculated from this surrogate. The 4 mm transverse and 2 mm axial stage travels are likewise display choices. Neither these dimensions nor the support bars predict a physical voxel. The resin is a zero-transmission thin sample plane (`transmitExc: true`, `transmission: 0`), which ends the unobserved writing light.

## Shared intensity frames and three experiments

The SLM displays a true **7×7 intensity grid**. The native tracer samples its center column (`maskSlice = 0.5`), whose default levels are `1, 0.72, 0, 0.38, 0.92, 0.18, 1`. Other columns vary to make the display genuinely two dimensional. This illustrative pattern is not a recovered experimental mask, and one sampled column does not reproduce the paper's full adaptive 2D printing. Values multiply intensity, not its square. No optical layers, geometric holographic orders or spectral-angle proxy are enabled on the SLM.

1. **Source gating:** turn off **Emit traced rays**, or set source power to 0 W. Writing disappears. Changing a positive 7 W to 3.5 W preserves normalized geometric weights and positions; source watts do not calibrate dose.
2. **Intensity selection:** on the SLM, use **Frame index** 0 for six selected regions, 1 for uniform illumination of all seven, and 2 for darkness. Frames 3–9 isolate input bands 0–6: they select local array channels M6–M0, respectively, preserving image inversion. Edit the JSON pixels or change **Traced column** to explore other sections. Optional discrete-frame playback is illustrative, not a calibrated update rate or laser synchronization.
3. **Focus and sample geometry:** increase the metalens focal length from 40 to 60 mm; seven channels remain, but their actual sample support broadens to more than 2.5 mm. Restore 40 mm, then select the stage's transverse or Z mode and play mechanics. Transverse motion moves sample coordinates through fixed foci; Z moves the actual sample plane and defocuses it. This is one transverse coordinate and one axial coordinate, not an independent XYZ raster.

The shared arrival preview produces one **actual sampled arrival plus its full support** per illuminated lenslet. It neither invents a focus at a centroid nor treats numerical source/spectral samples as additional writing channels. No vectorial high-NA field, meta-atom coupling, arbitrary CGH, phase-to-intensity calibration, air ionization, dose, threshold, curing, proximity effect or throughput is computed.
