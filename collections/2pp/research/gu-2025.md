This scene is a paper-grounded, editable mechanism reconstruction. The source-to-resin route is traced with native OpticalSetup components. It is not an exact optomechanical drawing or a calibrated two-photon-process model.

## Source-to-sample interpretation

The active writing path is Solstice → HWP1 → PBS1 → −100/+250 mm beam expander → PBS2 → QWP1 → reflective SLM → QWP1 → PBS2 output → QWP2 → L1 → vacuum-cell windows/focus → L2 → compact fold mirror → ML2 → resin/substrate on the moving stage. The fold matches Extended Data Fig. 2; exact free-space distances outside the reported focal-length relationships are not provided.

The workbench is drawn at a magnified apparatus scale. A real 100 µm, NA 0.8 lens has a microscale working distance that cannot be legibly combined with the metre-scale relay in one 2D view. The active metalens therefore uses a 40 mm display focal length. This preserves the visible causal relation—SLM amplitude selects beamlets, metalenses focus them, stage motion carries the resin—without claiming dimensional equivalence.

## Control experiments

1. **Laser emission:** select the Solstice and turn off **Emit traced rays**. Expected: the complete writing path and new voxel arrivals disappear. This verifies source enable/power behavior, not a damage or polymerization threshold.
2. **Spatial amplitude pattern:** select the LC-SLM and change the amplitude levels to `1,1,1,1,1,1,1`, then introduce zeros. Expected: representative beamlets become uniform, then selected slices vanish or dim. This verifies per-region illumination control; it does not reproduce the SLM’s 2D pixel grid or its calibrated `sin²(δ/2)` curve.
3. **Who makes the foci:** leave the SLM as an amplitude mask and increase the active ML2 focal length. Expected: the focal crossings move while the SLM creates no angular fan. This distinguishes the paper from holographic multifocus SLM systems. It does not predict a high-NA PSF.
4. **Synchronized substrate motion:** change the stage among **Static**, **XY**, and **XYZ sync**, and play mechanics. Expected: the resin/substrate moves through the fixed focal array and voxel markers follow sample coordinates. The displayed travel is enlarged; it is not the paper’s exact toolpath or speed.

## Model and handoff limits

- No vectorial high-NA field, meta-atom coupling, geometric-phase handedness efficiency, measured aberration, Strehl ratio, arbitrary 2D SLM image, spatial dose, voxel overlap, oxygen inhibition, depletion, cure threshold, or 3D polymerization kinetics is calculated.
- The vacuum cell is ordinary traced window glass around an empty region. Air-ionization suppression and nonlinear propagation are explanatory only.
- The observation objective is not used to focus the writing beam. The native trace does not synthesize reflected/scattered sample light back to the CMOS.
- The companion calculator accepts the reported 800 nm wavelength and active ML2 NA 0.8. It correctly omits 7,000 mW, 0.001 MHz and 35 fs because those values are outside its supported educational ranges. No per-focus/sample power is substituted.
