SLM holography · 2011

Fabrication of microscale medical devices by two-photon polymerization with multiple foci via a spatial light modulator

10.1364/BOE.2.003167

Current understanding

The SLM generates multiple independently located foci; the galvos move that pattern. The representative Venus array uses 4×4 foci, with 8–12 mW per spot and 1 mm/s scanning.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Chameleon: 780 nm, <150 fs, 80 MHz; 4 W source specification
  2. Liquid-crystal attenuator and PBS
  3. Beam expander
  4. Reflective LC-R2500 SLM with tiled CGH
  5. Fourier-plane order selection; zero order removed at plane P
  6. XY galvo scanner
  7. Microscope objective; representative 100×, NA 1.40 oil
  8. Resin on XYZ positioning stage

Observation and auxiliary paths

A CMOS camera branch connects near the scanner; transmission illumination is below the specimen. No APD is specified in Fig. 1.

Evidence inspected

  • PDF pp. 6–7, Fig. 1 on p. 7; fabrication examples pp. 8–10.

Limits and unresolved details

No CGH calculation, diffraction efficiency or 3D multi-focus field is solved. The 150 fs value is an upper bound and is deliberately not exported as an exact pulse duration.

  • Relay distances and lens focal lengths are not all specified.
  • 4 W source output is not 8–12 mW per-focus writing power.
  • Fig. 1 labels the attenuator as a half-wave plate/polarizer and the source as <140 fs; prose describes a liquid-crystal modulator/PBS and <150 fs. The drawing follows the prose and retains this discrepancy.
