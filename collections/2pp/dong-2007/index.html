Control experiments

  1. Laser enable: turn off the pulsed source; all traced light and every resin preview focus must disappear.
  2. Aperture selection: reduce the slit gap from 38 mm to 18 mm; the outer lenslets are blocked and the four-focus pattern becomes two central foci.
  3. Beam geometry: change the microlens count from 4 to 2; the same illuminated section resolves into two independently focused beamlets.
  4. Focus-plane mismatch: reduce lenslet focal length from 60 mm to 45 mm; the waist moves before the resin plane and the beamlets arrive diverging.

Current understanding

The abstract supports configurable lens sets and aperture masks that determine the geometry of multiple writing beams. Because the full text and apparatus figure remain unavailable, the working scene is an explicitly labelled 2D mechanism interpretation: a telescope expands a pulsed beam, a slit selects the illuminated array region, and independent lenslets form simultaneous foci on a moving resin plane.
