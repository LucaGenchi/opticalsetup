Current understanding

The abstract supports configurable lens sets and aperture masks that determine the geometry of multiple writing beams. Because the full text and apparatus figure remain unavailable, the working scene is an explicitly labelled 2D mechanism interpretation: a telescope expands a pulsed beam, a slit selects the illuminated array region, and independent lenslets form simultaneous foci on a moving resin plane.
