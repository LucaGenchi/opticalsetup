DOE parallel scanning · 2020

Rapid Assembly of Small Materials Building Blocks (Voxels) into Large Functional 3D Metamaterials

10.1002/adfm.201907795

Research retained · setup removed

The generated drawing has been removed. The source review below is preserved for a future individual reconstruction.

Current understanding

Nine foci share galvo scanning. A specifically chromatic telescope counteracts the angular dispersion of the DOE; a normal achromatic 1× telescope is not equivalent.

Reported optical sequence

  1. Mai Tai HP: 790 nm, 90 fs, 80 MHz, 2.8 W
  2. AOM: first order writing, zero order dumped
  3. Two N-SF10 prisms: 2 m tip spacing, approximately −30000 fs² compensation
  4. 3×3 static DOE at entrance pupil of dispersion-compensating telescope
  5. Dispersion-compensating telescope (DCT), near-unity magnification
  6. PBS, LG1/LG2 demagnifying relay, GX
  7. LG3/LG4 relay, GY
  8. LG5/LG6 relay; dichroic reflects 790 nm
  9. Quarter-wave plate; Zeiss 40× NA 1.4 oil
  10. Resin, coarse XY and piezo Z stages

Observation and auxiliary paths

LED transmission imaging goes to a camera. Sample backreflection double-passes the QWP, changes polarization and exits the PBS toward an APD.

Limits and unresolved details

The DCT is labelled as a prescription-dependent assembly; no ordinary relay is claimed to reproduce its chromatic correction. AOM frequency and instantaneous gate timing are separate quantities.

  • Detailed lens prescriptions require the cited instrument sources.
  • 2.8 W source output is outside the destination power input range.
