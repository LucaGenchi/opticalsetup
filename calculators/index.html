Calculators

Each calculator is one physical model on one page: set the parameters, read the results and graphs, then the physics behind them, with every formula, its assumptions, how precisely it is evaluated and the literature it comes from. Material models reuse the canvas’s catalogue and equations; wavelength converters evaluate frequency relations directly. Each page states its scope and reference checks.

Material dispersionRefractive and group index, GVD and TOD for optical glasses, crystals, water and air; GDD, TOD and pulse stretching for a laser bandwidth. Nonlinear frequency conversionSHG, THG, SFG, DFG, OPO, CARS and CSRS wavelengths from energy conservation, with frequencies, photon energies and a Gaussian bandwidth estimate for each generated wave. Coherent Raman scatteringRaman shift, pump or Stokes from the other two; CARS and CSRS wavelengths; spectral resolution; the probed window on measured reference spectra. Dispersion compensationPrism pairs, four-prism lines, grating pairs and a grating stretcher: GDD, TOD and FOD from the geometry, the separation that cancels a chirp, the output pulse, and the bench drawn to scale as you change it. FluorescenceLinear (one-photon) or two-photon excited fluorescence of fluorescent proteins, on their measured spectra: excitation efficiency, emission through filters and a detector, crosstalk, source blocking and saturation. Point-scanning resolution and field of viewLateral resolution of a scanned focus, and axial resolution where the signal is confined in depth: with a confocal pinhole or with two- and three-photon excitation; field of view and the spot displacement of a galvo mirror. Thin and thick lensFocus a laser with an ideal thin lens or a real singlet, given by its focal length or its radii: the focused beam and spot, and colour. Imaging with lensesWhere a lens images an object, thin or thick, real or virtual, drawn to scale; and two lenses as a Keplerian or Galilean telescope, a beam expander or a 4f relay. Lens designEnter a lens prescription and trace real rays in 3D: layout, spot diagrams, Seidel aberrations, wavefront maps, the diffraction PSF, Strehl ratio and MTF, images in coherent and incoherent light, and fiber coupling. Gaussian beamWaist, Rayleigh range, divergence and beam size at any distance; focusing by a thin lens, with M². Free space to fiber couplingCouple a collimated laser beam into a single-mode fiber: the lens that matches the mode, a plano-convex lens to start from, and real singlets traced with their aberrations, against lateral, axial and angular offset. Optical parametric amplifier (OPA)Seeded single-pass parametric gain from pump intensity, crystal and pulse timing, with the idler and the pump depletion.
Calculators evaluate optical models, reuse workbench functions where available, and state how each was checked. They are not a substitute for design software: read each page’s precision section before relying on a number. See the validation table, or open the canvas.
