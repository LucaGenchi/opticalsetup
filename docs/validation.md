# Validation of the quantitative models

Generated by `python3 validation/run.py`; do not edit by hand. Each row is a
model the app ships, checked by `test/validation.test.js` against an
independent reference implementation under `validation/reference/`. The
tolerance is the relative (or, where marked, absolute) agreement the test
demands; the reference files state the equations, conventions and sources.

A model listed here is **computed**: the number the app shows is the number
the reference produces, inside the stated scope. Outside that scope the app
reports the quantity as unavailable rather than extrapolating. Models that
are not listed here are qualitative or diagram-only; see the wiki page of
each component and the simulation-scope section of the README.

See `docs/adding-physics.md` for how a new model earns a row in this table.

## Sources

**Catalogue glass dispersion (Sellmeier)**

- Schott AG, optical glass data sheets (N-BK7, N-SF5, N-SF11)
- I. H. Malitson, J. Opt. Soc. Am. 55, 1205 (1965), fused silica

**Transform limit, quadratic-phase stretching, autocorrelation factors**

- J.-C. Diels and W. Rudolph, Ultrashort Laser Pulse Phenomena, 2nd ed., ch. 1 (time-bandwidth products, autocorrelation factors)
- G. P. Agrawal, Nonlinear Fiber Optics, ch. 3 (Gaussian pulse broadening under GVD)

**Argon hollow capillary: dispersion, Kerr coefficient, ideal loss**

- E. R. Peck and D. J. Fisher, J. Opt. Soc. Am. 54, 1362 (1964), argon refractivity
- E. A. J. Marcatili and R. A. Schmeltzer, Bell Syst. Tech. J. 43, 1783 (1964), hollow dielectric waveguides
- S. Zahedpour, J. K. Wahlstrand and H. M. Milchberg, Opt. Lett. 40, 5794 (2015), argon n2

**Scalar envelope propagation: GDD, Kerr SPM and loss (hollow-core solver)**

- G. P. Agrawal, Nonlinear Fiber Optics, 5th ed., sections 2.3 and 4.1 (NLSE, SPM broadening)

**Thick singlet cardinal points, Stokes retarder and analyzer, etalon finesse**

- E. Hecht, Optics, 5th ed., section 6.2 (thick lens), section 8.13 (Stokes parameters and Mueller matrices)
- D. H. Goldstein, Polarized Light, 3rd ed., ch. 6 (Mueller matrices of retarders and polarizers)
- M. Born and E. Wolf, Principles of Optics, section 7.6 (Fabry-Perot finesse)
